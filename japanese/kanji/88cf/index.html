Kanji 裏

back, amidst, in

裏

裏 is a kanji taught in grade 6 of Japanese elementary school. It is written with 13 strokes. In KANJIDIC2 its English meaning is back, amidst, in, reverse, inside.

Its classical radical is number 145 (⾐, "Clothes"), the part that dictionaries use to look it up. Other grade 6 kanji with the same radical: 裁, 装, 補.

It is number 812 in a ranking of the 2,500 kanji most used in newspapers. KANJIDIC2 puts it at level 2 of the old four-level JLPT scale (1 is the hardest). Both are statistics from the file; see reading kanji for how to use them.

Among grade 6 kanji

Grade 6 has 191 kanji; 15 of them (including this one) have 13 strokes, which is more than the middle of the grade. Others with 13 strokes: 絹, 源, 署, 傷, 蒸, 聖, 誠, 暖, 腸, 賃, 腹, 幕.

Readings

On readings (from Chinese) are in katakana; kun readings (Japanese) are in hiragana, with a dot before the part that is written after the kanji (okurigana). The small letters are romaji.
