KANJIDIC2 uses the four-level scale of the Japanese Language Proficiency Test from before 2010, where level 1 was the hardest. The test now has five levels (N1 to N5) and its organisers publish no official kanji lists, so this list does not say what is on the current N5, N4 or other level. It only includes kanji of the 2,136 jōyō set.
