Three kinds of writing

Japanese is written with three sets of signs at once: hiragana and katakana, which are sound signs, and kanji, which came from Chinese characters and carry meaning. How Japanese is written explains how they fit together, how to learn kana gives a plan, and reading kanji explains on and kun readings.

The typer

The box above turns romaji into kana by Hepburn spelling. Write a double consonant for a small tsu (kitte becomes きって) and "nn" or "n'" for a ん before a vowel. It is a learning tool, not a full input method.
