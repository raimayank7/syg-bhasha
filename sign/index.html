Indian Sign Language: a plain guide for beginners

What ISL is, how to talk with Deaf people, and where to learn the language free from official videos. Written for families, students, teachers and staff who hear.

Please read this first. This site does not print pictures of signs or an alphabet chart. A sign is a movement of hands, face and body, and we would not publish hand shapes that we cannot vouch for. Instead we explain the language in words and send you to the video dictionary and free course of ISLRTC, the Government of India's centre for Indian Sign Language, and to Deaf teachers. Five things to know about ISL

  1. It is a full language. India's national centre for it describes ISL as a visual manual language with distinct grammar and vocabulary. It is not Hindi or English on the hands.
  2. It is Indian. American and British sign languages are different languages. Most videos and apps that a quick search finds teach American signs.
  3. The face is part of it. Eyebrows, head and body carry grammar. Watch the whole signer.
  4. The alphabet is a small part. Fingerspelling is used mainly for names. Learning ISL means learning signs and sentences.
  5. You can begin free, today. ISLRTC offers a video dictionary and an online basic course in ten modules.

Who this site is for

For the parent who has just learnt that a child is deaf. For the student with a Deaf classmate, and the teacher with a Deaf student. For the colleague, the bank clerk, the nurse, the shopkeeper and the bus conductor who meet Deaf people at work and want to do better than shouting or giving up. For anyone who has watched the interpreter in the corner of a news broadcast and wondered how the language works. It is a first step. The language itself must be learnt from the people whose language it is.

How to use it

Read what ISL is and myths and facts to clear away wrong ideas. Read communicating with a Deaf person before your next meeting. Then follow how to learn ISL, with the checklist open beside the official dictionary. Hindi readers can begin with the हिंदी पन्ना.
