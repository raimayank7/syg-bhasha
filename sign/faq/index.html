Questions people ask about ISL

Short answers to the questions hearing people ask most. Where an answer depends on an official body, we say which, and we do not guess.

Learning

How long does it take to learn ISL?

Enough to greet, introduce yourself and handle simple exchanges: a few weeks of daily practice, for example with ISLRTC's ten-module basic course. To hold a real conversation: many months, with regular contact with Deaf signers. To interpret: a two-year diploma and years of practice. It is a language, and it takes about as long as any other.

Can I learn ISL free?

Yes. ISLRTC offers a free online basic course in self-learning mode and a free video dictionary. See how to learn ISL.

Where is the ISL alphabet chart?

We do not publish one, because we could not be sure of drawing every hand shape correctly, and a wrong chart teaches a wrong alphabet. Learn the letters from the official videos. See fingerspelling in ISL.

Can I learn from American Sign Language videos?

No. American Sign Language is a different language with different signs and a one-handed alphabet. Check that your material says "Indian Sign Language".

I am left-handed. Does it matter?

No. Use your left hand as the active hand and keep to it.

Am I too old to learn?

No. Adults learn ISL all the time: parents, grandparents, teachers, colleagues. Fingers may be stiff at first; they loosen with practice.

The language

Is ISL the same across India?

ISLRTC states that ISL is used in the deaf community all over India. Some signs differ by region, as words differ between dialects, and signers generally understand each other.

Is ISL an official language of India?

We have not verified the present legal position from an official source, so we do not state one here. What we can say from its website is that the Government of India set up a national centre for the language, ISLRTC, in 2015, and that it works to promote ISL in education and public life.

How many people use ISL?

There is no exact count of signers that we can point to. ISLRTC's About page cites the 2011 Census figure of about 50 lakh deaf persons in India; not all of them sign, and many hearing people do.

Does ISL have grammar?

Yes, its own. See what ISL is.

Courses and work

What qualification does an interpreter need?

ISLRTC runs a two-year Diploma in Indian Sign Language Interpretation (DISLI), which its website says is approved by the Rehabilitation Council of India. See courses and careers.

What are the fees and the last date for admission?

They change every year. We do not copy them. Read the current admission notice on islrtc.nic.in.

How can I find an interpreter?

ISLRTC publishes a directory of ISL interpreters on its website. Local Deaf associations also know who works in your city.

Meeting Deaf people

What should I do if I do not know any signs?

Face the person, ask how they would like to communicate, and write. See communicating with a Deaf person.

Is it rude to watch people signing?

Staring at a private conversation is rude in any language. Showing friendly interest and asking to learn a sign is usually welcome.

Which word should I use: deaf, hearing impaired or something else?

"Deaf" and "hard of hearing" are the most widely accepted. Avoid "deaf and dumb". See words and Deaf culture.

This website

Is this an official website?

No. It is an independent guide by ShareYourGroup. It is not connected with ISLRTC or any government body. Official information is at islrtc.nic.in.

Why are there no pictures of signs here?

Because a sign is a movement of hands, face and body that a drawing cannot show reliably, and because we will not publish hand shapes we cannot vouch for. Video from the national centre does the job properly.

Was this site written by Deaf people?

No, and you should know that. It was written by hearing people from public sources, to help other hearing people take a first step. For the language itself, go to Deaf teachers.
