Common spoken English mistakes, and how to fix them

Most mistakes of Indian speakers are not random. They come from carrying a Hindi or Punjabi pattern into English, word for word. Once you see the pattern, the mistake corrects itself. This page lists thirty of the commonest, grouped by cause.

A fair warning first: several of these forms are so widely used in India that everyone here understands them, and Indian English is a real variety of the language. But they can confuse a listener from another country and are marked wrong in examinations and interviews. It is worth knowing the standard form.

1. "-ing" where English uses the simple form

Hindi says "मेरे पास है", "मुझे पता है" with a continuous feel, and speakers carry it over. In English, verbs of having, knowing, liking and understanding normally stay simple.

Often saidStandard English
I am having two brothers.I have two brothers.
I am knowing the answer.I know the answer.
She is liking this song.She likes this song.
I am not understanding.I do not understand.
He is owning a shop.He owns a shop.

2. An extra word that English does not need

Often saidStandard EnglishWhy
Let us discuss about it.Let us discuss it."Discuss" already means talk about.
Please return back my book.Please return my book."Return" includes "back".
I will revert back to you.I will get back to you."Revert" means go back to an earlier state.
Repeat it again.Repeat it."Repeat" is already "again".
She is my cousin sister.She is my cousin.English "cousin" covers both.
I am fine only.I am fine."Only" here copies Hindi "ही".
Myself Rahul.I am Rahul."Myself" cannot begin a sentence like this.

3. The wrong small word (preposition)

Often saidStandard English
I am angry on you.I am angry with you.
She is married with a doctor.She is married to a doctor.
I will explain you.I will explain it to you.
He told to me.He told me.
I came by walk.I came on foot. / I walked.
We reached to Delhi.We reached Delhi.
The bank is opposite to the school.The bank is opposite the school.

4. Questions without the question shape

Hindi makes a question with the voice alone: "तुम आ रहे हो?" English must move or add a helping verb.

Often saidStandard English
You are coming?Are you coming?
What you are doing?What are you doing?
Where he lives?Where does he live?
You went there, no?You went there, didn't you?
He is your brother, isn't it?He is your brother, isn't he?

5. Words used differently in India

Often saidStandard English
I passed out of college in 2024.I graduated in 2024. ("Pass out" means faint.)
What is your good name?May I know your name?
Please do the needful.Please do what is needed.
The meeting is preponed.The meeting has been brought forward.
I gave my exam yesterday.I took my exam yesterday. (The teacher gives it.)
My head is paining.I have a headache. / My head hurts.

How to drop a mistake for good

  1. Pick only three mistakes from this page that you know you make.
  2. Write the correct sentence for each on a slip of paper and say it aloud five times every morning for a week.
  3. Ask a friend or family member to raise a finger whenever you slip. No scolding; just the finger.
  4. When those three are gone, pick the next three.
