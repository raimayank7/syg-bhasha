English sentences: small talk: weather, time and plans

15 sentences you can use today, with Hindi and Punjabi meaning, a short dialogue and speaking tips. Tap Listen to hear a sentence.

Small talk is the light conversation people make at the bus stop, in the lift or over the wall with a neighbour. It is not about anything important, and that is the point: it keeps relations warm. In English, the safest subjects are the weather, the day, work or studies in general, food, festivals and cricket.

For the weather, English uses "It is": it is hot, it is raining, it is very cold today. For time, also "It is": it is half past five, it is quarter to nine. For plans, use "going to": I am going to visit my aunt on Sunday.

Subjects to keep away from with people you do not know well are salary, age, religion, politics and personal questions such as "Why are you not married?" A good small-talk question is open and easy: "How was your weekend?", "Any plans for Diwali?", "Did you watch the match?"

Sentences to learn

  1. It is very hot today, isn't it?

    आज बहुत गर्मी है, है न?

    ਅੱਜ ਬੜੀ ਗਰਮੀ ਹੈ, ਹੈ ਨਾ?

  2. It looks like it is going to rain.

    लगता है बारिश होने वाली है।

    ਲੱਗਦਾ ਹੈ ਮੀਂਹ ਪੈਣ ਵਾਲਾ ਹੈ।

  3. The weather is pleasant this morning.

    आज सुबह मौसम सुहावना है।

    ਅੱਜ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਮੌਸਮ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਾ ਹੈ।

  4. What time is it, please?

    कितने बजे हैं?

    ਕਿੰਨੇ ਵੱਜੇ ਨੇ ਜੀ?

  5. It is half past five.

    साढ़े पाँच बजे हैं।

    ਸਾਢੇ ਪੰਜ ਵੱਜੇ ਨੇ।

  6. How was your weekend?

    आपका सप्ताहांत कैसा रहा?

    ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਵੀਕਐਂਡ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਰਿਹਾ?

  7. It was good. We went to my village.

    अच्छा रहा। हम अपने गाँव गए थे।

    ਚੰਗਾ ਰਿਹਾ। ਅਸੀਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਗਏ ਸੀ।

  8. Any plans for Diwali?

    दिवाली पर क्या कार्यक्रम है?

    ਦੀਵਾਲੀ ਤੇ ਕੀ ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ ਹੈ?

  9. I am going to visit my sister on Sunday.

    मैं रविवार को अपनी बहन के घर जाने वाला हूँ।

    ਮੈਂ ਐਤਵਾਰ ਨੂੰ ਆਪਣੀ ਭੈਣ ਦੇ ਘਰ ਜਾਣਾ ਹੈ।

  10. Did you watch the match last night?

    क्या आपने कल रात का मैच देखा?

    ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਰਾਤ ਦਾ ਮੈਚ ਵੇਖਿਆ?

  11. How are the children doing in their studies?

    बच्चों की पढ़ाई कैसी चल रही है?

    ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਪੜ੍ਹਾਈ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਚੱਲ ਰਹੀ ਹੈ?

  12. The traffic was terrible this morning.

    आज सुबह बहुत ज़्यादा ट्रैफ़िक था।

    ਅੱਜ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਬਹੁਤ ਟ੍ਰੈਫ਼ਿਕ ਸੀ।

  13. Are you free this evening?

    क्या आप आज शाम ख़ाली हैं?

    ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਅੱਜ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਵਿਹਲੇ ਹੋ?

  14. Let us meet for tea sometime.

    कभी चाय पर मिलते हैं।

    ਕਦੇ ਚਾਹ ਤੇ ਮਿਲਦੇ ਹਾਂ।

  15. Have a nice day!

    आपका दिन शुभ हो!

    ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਦਿਨ ਚੰਗਾ ਲੰਘੇ!

A short dialogue

Read it aloud twice, once as each speaker. Then cover the English and try to say it from the Hindi.

Neighbour: Good morning! It is quite cold today, isn't it?सुप्रभात! आज काफ़ी ठंड है, है न?

You: Yes, winter has really arrived. How was your weekend?हाँ, सर्दी सचमुच आ गई। आपका सप्ताहांत कैसा रहा?

Neighbour: Very nice. My son came home from the hostel.बहुत अच्छा। मेरा बेटा हॉस्टल से घर आया था।

You: That is lovely. How is he doing in his studies?बहुत बढ़िया। उसकी पढ़ाई कैसी चल रही है?

Neighbour: Quite well. Come over for tea this evening.काफ़ी अच्छी। आज शाम चाय पर आइए।

You: Sure, thank you. See you at five.ज़रूर, धन्यवाद। पाँच बजे मिलते हैं।

Speak it right

  • The tag "isn't it?" fits only "It is..." sentences. After "You are coming", the tag is "aren't you?", not "isn't it?"
  • Say "half past five" and "quarter to nine". "Five thirty" is also fine.
  • Say "I am going to my village", not "I am going to my native".

Practise this page

  1. Listen to each sentence and repeat it aloud, three times.
  2. Change one word to make it true for you: your name, your town, your work.
  3. Use at least two of these sentences with a real person today.
