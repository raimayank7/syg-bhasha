English sentences: at a restaurant

16 sentences you can use today, with Hindi and Punjabi meaning, a short dialogue and speaking tips. Tap Listen to hear a sentence.

Eating out has a clear order: you get a table, read the menu, order, eat, and pay. Each step has two or three standard sentences, and waiters everywhere expect them.

The key to ordering politely is "I would like" (often shortened to "I'd like") and "Could I have". "I want a dosa" is correct grammar but sounds blunt. "I would like a masala dosa, please" sounds pleasant. For the whole table, say "We would like..." or "Could we have...?"

If something is wrong, complain calmly and state the fact: "Excuse me, this is cold" or "I ordered a plain naan, not a butter naan." Add "Could you change it, please?" At the end, the sentence is "Could we have the bill, please?" In India people also say "cheque"; "bill" is understood everywhere.

Sentences to learn

  1. A table for four, please.

    चार लोगों के लिए एक टेबल चाहिए।

    ਚਾਰ ਬੰਦਿਆਂ ਲਈ ਇੱਕ ਟੇਬਲ ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ।

  2. Could we see the menu, please?

    क्या हमें मेन्यू मिल सकता है?

    ਕੀ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਮੀਨੂ ਮਿਲ ਸਕਦਾ ਹੈ?

  3. What do you recommend?

    आप क्या खाने की सलाह देंगे?

    ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੀ ਖਾਣ ਦੀ ਸਲਾਹ ਦਿਓਗੇ?

  4. I would like a masala dosa, please.

    मुझे एक मसाला डोसा चाहिए।

    ਮੈਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਮਸਾਲਾ ਡੋਸਾ ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ।

  5. Is this dish very spicy?

    क्या यह बहुत तीखा है?

    ਕੀ ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਤਿੱਖਾ ਹੈ?

  6. Please make it less spicy.

    इसे कम तीखा बनाइएगा।

    ਇਹਨੂੰ ਘੱਟ ਤਿੱਖਾ ਬਣਾਉਣਾ।

  7. I am a vegetarian.

    मैं शाकाहारी हूँ।

    ਮੈਂ ਸ਼ਾਕਾਹਾਰੀ ਹਾਂ।

  8. Does this have onion or garlic?

    क्या इसमें प्याज़ या लहसुन है?

    ਕੀ ਇਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਿਆਜ਼ ਜਾਂ ਲਸਣ ਹੈ?

  9. Could we have some water, please?

    हमें थोड़ा पानी मिल सकता है?

    ਸਾਨੂੰ ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਮਿਲ ਸਕਦਾ ਹੈ?

  10. How long will the order take?

    ऑर्डर आने में कितना समय लगेगा?

    ਆਰਡਰ ਆਉਣ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਿੰਨਾ ਸਮਾਂ ਲੱਗੇਗਾ?

  11. Excuse me, this is not what I ordered.

    माफ़ कीजिए, मैंने यह ऑर्डर नहीं किया था।

    ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰਨਾ, ਮੈਂ ਇਹ ਆਰਡਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਕੀਤਾ ਸੀ।

  12. The food is cold. Could you warm it, please?

    खाना ठंडा है। क्या आप इसे गरम कर देंगे?

    ਖਾਣਾ ਠੰਢਾ ਹੈ। ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਇਹਨੂੰ ਗਰਮ ਕਰ ਦਿਓਗੇ?

  13. One more roti, please.

    एक रोटी और दीजिए।

    ਇੱਕ ਰੋਟੀ ਹੋਰ ਦੇ ਦਿਓ।

  14. Could you pack the rest?

    बचा हुआ खाना पैक कर देंगे?

    ਬਚਿਆ ਖਾਣਾ ਪੈਕ ਕਰ ਦਿਓਗੇ?

  15. Could we have the bill, please?

    बिल ले आइए।

    ਬਿੱਲ ਲੈ ਆਓ ਜੀ।

  16. The food was very good. Thank you.

    खाना बहुत अच्छा था। धन्यवाद।

    ਖਾਣਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਵਧੀਆ ਸੀ। ਧੰਨਵਾਦ।

A short dialogue

Read it aloud twice, once as each speaker. Then cover the English and try to say it from the Hindi.

Waiter: Good evening. Are you ready to order?नमस्ते। क्या आप ऑर्डर देंगे?

Guest: Yes. We would like one paneer butter masala and four rotis.जी। हमें एक पनीर बटर मसाला और चार रोटी चाहिए।

Waiter: Anything to drink?पीने के लिए कुछ?

Guest: Two sweet lassis, please. And please make the curry less spicy.दो मीठी लस्सी। और सब्ज़ी कम तीखी रखिएगा।

Waiter: Sure. It will take fifteen minutes.ज़रूर। पंद्रह मिनट लगेंगे।

Guest: That is fine. Thank you.ठीक है। धन्यवाद।

Speak it right

  • "I would like..." is the polite way to order. "Give me..." sounds rude to a waiter.
  • Say "less spicy", not "less chilly". "Chilly" means cold; "chilli" is the spice.
  • Say "Could you pack the rest?" In India "parcel it" is common but not standard elsewhere.

Practise this page

  1. Listen to each sentence and repeat it aloud, three times.
  2. Change one word to make it true for you: your name, your town, your work.
  3. Use at least two of these sentences with a real person today.
