English sentences: guests and visiting

15 sentences you can use today, with Hindi and Punjabi meaning, a short dialogue and speaking tips. Tap Listen to hear a sentence.

Indian homes take pride in looking after guests, and the same warmth can be shown in English. This page has the sentences of a visit in the order they happen: the welcome at the door, the offer of water and tea, the meal, and the goodbye.

Offers in English use "Would you like...?" and "Will you have...?" The guest answers "Yes, please" to accept and "No, thank you" to refuse. A plain "No" sounds cold, and "Yes" without "please" sounds like an order to the host. English speakers also do not press food as often as we do; one or two offers are polite, and "Are you sure?" is the gentle way to ask again.

When you are the guest, praise something simple and true ("You have a lovely home", "The tea is very good") and thank the host twice: once when leaving and once the next day by message.

Sentences to learn

  1. Welcome! Please come in.

    स्वागत है! अंदर आइए।

    ਜੀ ਆਇਆਂ ਨੂੰ! ਅੰਦਰ ਆਓ।

  2. Did you find the house easily?

    घर ढूँढ़ने में कोई दिक़्क़त तो नहीं हुई?

    ਘਰ ਲੱਭਣ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੋਈ ਦਿੱਕਤ ਤਾਂ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੋਈ?

  3. Please have a seat.

    बैठिए।

    ਬੈਠੋ ਜੀ।

  4. Make yourself comfortable.

    आराम से बैठिए, अपना ही घर समझिए।

    ਅਰਾਮ ਨਾਲ ਬੈਠੋ, ਆਪਣਾ ਹੀ ਘਰ ਸਮਝੋ।

  5. Would you like tea or coffee?

    आप चाय लेंगे या कॉफ़ी?

    ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਲਵੋਗੇ ਜਾਂ ਕੌਫ਼ੀ?

  6. Yes, please. Tea without sugar.

    जी हाँ। बिना चीनी की चाय।

    ਹਾਂ ਜੀ। ਬਿਨਾਂ ਖੰਡ ਦੀ ਚਾਹ।

  7. No, thank you. I have just had some.

    नहीं, धन्यवाद। मैंने अभी-अभी पी है।

    ਨਹੀਂ, ਧੰਨਵਾਦ। ਮੈਂ ਹੁਣੇ ਪੀਤੀ ਹੈ।

  8. Please help yourself.

    आप ख़ुद लीजिए, संकोच मत कीजिए।

    ਆਪੇ ਲੈ ਲਓ, ਸੰਗੋ ਨਾ।

  9. Please have a little more.

    थोड़ा और लीजिए।

    ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ ਹੋਰ ਲਓ।

  10. I am full, thank you. It was delicious.

    मेरा पेट भर गया, धन्यवाद। बहुत स्वादिष्ट था।

    ਮੇਰਾ ਢਿੱਡ ਭਰ ਗਿਆ, ਧੰਨਵਾਦ। ਬਹੁਤ ਸੁਆਦ ਸੀ।

  11. You have a lovely home.

    आपका घर बहुत सुंदर है।

    ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਘਰ ਬਹੁਤ ਸੋਹਣਾ ਹੈ।

  12. Please stay for dinner.

    रात का खाना खाकर जाइए।

    ਰਾਤ ਦੀ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾ ਕੇ ਜਾਣਾ।

  13. It is getting late. We should leave now.

    देर हो रही है। अब हमें चलना चाहिए।

    ਦੇਰ ਹੋ ਰਹੀ ਹੈ। ਹੁਣ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਚੱਲਣਾ ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ।

  14. Thank you for coming. Please visit again.

    आने के लिए धन्यवाद। फिर आइएगा।

    ਆਉਣ ਲਈ ਧੰਨਵਾਦ। ਫੇਰ ਆਉਣਾ।

  15. We had a wonderful time.

    हमें बहुत अच्छा लगा।

    ਸਾਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗਾ ਲੱਗਾ।

A short dialogue

Read it aloud twice, once as each speaker. Then cover the English and try to say it from the Hindi.

Host: Welcome! Please come in. Did you find the house easily?स्वागत है! अंदर आइए। घर आसानी से मिल गया?

Guest: Yes, your directions were very clear.जी, आपने रास्ता बहुत अच्छे से बताया था।

Host: Please have a seat. Would you like tea or something cold?बैठिए। चाय लेंगे या कुछ ठंडा?

Guest: Tea, please. With a little sugar.चाय, थोड़ी चीनी के साथ।

Host: Please have some snacks too.कुछ नाश्ता भी लीजिए।

Guest: Thank you. You have a lovely home.धन्यवाद। आपका घर बहुत सुंदर है।

Speak it right

  • Accept with "Yes, please" and refuse with "No, thank you".
  • Say "Please have a seat" or "Please sit down". "Please be seated" is very formal.
  • Say "I am full", not "My stomach is full".

Practise this page

  1. Listen to each sentence and repeat it aloud, three times.
  2. Change one word to make it true for you: your name, your town, your work.
  3. Use at least two of these sentences with a real person today.
