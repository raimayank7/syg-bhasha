Spoken English

A 30 day spoken English plan

This plan takes about twenty minutes a day. Each day has three parts: learn (one page or part of a page), say (speak the sentences aloud, with the Listen button where there is one), and use (a small real-life task). Tick each day in a notebook. If you miss a day, do not double up; simply carry on.

Week 1: The basics of talking to people

DayLearnUse
1Greetings and introductionsIntroduce yourself to the mirror in five sentences.
2Please, sorry and thank youUse "please" and "thank you" in English ten times today.
3At home, first eight sentencesSpeak only English at breakfast.
4At home, the restGive three household requests in English.
5Think in EnglishName twenty things in your house in English.
6Tenses: simple present and present continuousDescribe what each family member is doing right now.
7Revise days 1 to 6Record one minute: "My family". Keep the recording.

Week 2: Out of the house

DayLearnUse
8At the shop and marketAsk one price in English at a shop.
9DirectionsExplain the way from your house to the nearest bus stop.
10Bus, train and autoSay aloud how you travel to work or school.
11Questions: patterns 1 and 2Ask a family member ten questions in English.
12Tenses: simple pastTell someone what you did yesterday, in ten sentences.
13At a restaurantOrder your next tea or snack in English, even at home.
14Revise days 8 to 13Record one minute: "What I did this week".

Week 3: Services and the phone

DayLearnUse
15On the phoneMake one short call to a friend in English.
16At the doctor'sDescribe the last time you were unwell.
17At the bank and post officeSay what you would ask at the bank counter.
18Sentence patterns 1 to 10Make three sentences of your own with each.
19Sentence patterns 11 to 20Make three sentences of your own with each.
20Tenses: present perfect and futureSay five things you have done today and five you will do tomorrow.
21Revise days 15 to 20Record one minute: "My plans for next month".

Week 4: People, work and polish

DayLearnUse
22Guests and visitingOffer tea to someone in English.
23Small talkTalk about the weather with a neighbour or colleague.
24Feelings and opinionsGive your opinion on a film or a match in five sentences.
25At the office or at schoolUse three of the sentences at work or in class.
26Common mistakesPick your three mistakes and say the correct forms five times.
27Pronunciation: v/w, z, thRead the practice words to the mirror.
28Job interviewAnswer "Tell me about yourself" aloud, in six sentences.
29Hotel and trip, emergenciesPlan an imaginary trip aloud.
30Revise the whole monthRecord "My family" again. Compare with day 7.

Rules that make the plan work

  • Aloud, always. Reading the sentences silently does not count.
  • Same time every day. Tie it to a habit you already have, such as morning tea.
  • Mistakes are the work. If you made none, the task was too easy.
  • Keep the three recordings of days 7, 14 and 21, and the pair of days 7 and 30. Your own voice is the proof of progress.

After day 30

Repeat the plan with a twist: this time, after each situation page, write and speak a new dialogue of your own for it. Then move outwards: an English newspaper column each day, and five minutes of English conversation with one real person. Fluency is not a finish line that you cross. It is a habit that you keep.
