Twenty sentence patterns that make a thousand sentences

Fluent speakers do not build each sentence from single words. They carry a stock of ready frames and drop new words into them. Learn the twenty frames on this page and you can say a thousand useful sentences. For each, the Hindi and Punjabi sense is given, then three examples. Make three more of your own, aloud.

1. I would like to ...

मैं ... चाहूँगा · ਮੈਂ ... ਚਾਹਾਂਗਾ

I would like to book a ticket. I would like to meet the manager. I would like to say something.

2. Could you please ...?

क्या आप ... कर देंगे? · ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ... ਕਰ ਦਿਓਗੇ?

Could you please open the window? Could you please speak slowly? Could you please send me the photo?

3. Let us ...

चलो ... करें · ਚੱਲੋ ... ਕਰੀਏ

Let us go for a walk. Let us start the meeting. Let us ask someone.

4. I have to ...

मुझे ... करना है / पड़ेगा · ਮੈਨੂੰ ... ਕਰਨਾ ਪੈਣਾ ਹੈ

I have to leave now. I have to pay the bill today. I have to get up early tomorrow.

5. I used to ...

मैं पहले ... करता था · ਮੈਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ... ਕਰਦਾ ਸੀ

I used to play cricket. I used to live in a village. I used to drink a lot of tea.

6. I am going to ...

मैं ... करने वाला हूँ · ਮੈਂ ... ਕਰਨ ਵਾਲਾ ਹਾਂ

I am going to buy a scooter. I am going to call him tonight. I am going to learn driving.

7. Do you mind if I ...?

अगर मैं ... करूँ तो आपको एतराज़ तो नहीं? · ਜੇ ਮੈਂ ... ਕਰਾਂ ਤਾਂ ਤੁਹਾਨੂੰ ਇਤਰਾਜ਼ ਤਾਂ ਨਹੀਂ?

Do you mind if I sit here? Do you mind if I open the door? Do you mind if I call you later?

8. How about ...?

... कैसा रहेगा? · ... ਕਿਵੇਂ ਰਹੇਗਾ?

How about a cup of tea? How about meeting on Sunday? How about asking your father?

9. I am looking for ...

मैं ... ढूँढ़ रहा हूँ · ਮੈਂ ... ਲੱਭ ਰਿਹਾ ਹਾਂ

I am looking for my glasses. I am looking for a room on rent. I am looking for platform four.

10. I am good at ... / I am not good at ...

मैं ... में अच्छा हूँ · ਮੈਂ ... ਵਿੱਚ ਚੰਗਾ ਹਾਂ

I am good at maths. She is good at cooking. I am not good at remembering names.

11. It is time to ...

... का समय हो गया · ... ਦਾ ਵੇਲਾ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ

It is time to go. It is time to take your medicine. It is time to start.

12. There is ... / There are ...

... है / ... हैं (कहीं कुछ होना) · ... ਹੈ / ... ਨੇ

There is a bank near my house. There are two parks in our colony. There is no milk in the fridge.

13. I was about to ...

मैं ... करने ही वाला था · ਮੈਂ ... ਕਰਨ ਹੀ ਵਾਲਾ ਸੀ

I was about to call you. I was about to leave. I was about to say the same thing.

14. You should ... / You should not ...

तुम्हें ... करना चाहिए · ਤੈਨੂੰ ... ਕਰਨਾ ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ

You should see a doctor. You should save some money. You should not skip breakfast.

15. I feel like ...

मेरा ... करने का मन है · ਮੇਰਾ ... ਕਰਨ ਨੂੰ ਜੀ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ

I feel like having an ice cream. I feel like sleeping. I do not feel like going out.

16. Have you ever ...?

क्या तुमने कभी ... किया है? · ਕੀ ਤੂੰ ਕਦੇ ... ਕੀਤਾ ਹੈ?

Have you ever been to Shimla? Have you ever seen snow? Have you ever cooked for ten people?

17. As soon as ...

जैसे ही ... · ਜਿਵੇਂ ਹੀ ...

As soon as I reach, I will call you. As soon as the bell rang, the children ran out. Tell me as soon as you know.

18. I am used to ...

मुझे ... की आदत है · ਮੈਨੂੰ ... ਦੀ ਆਦਤ ਹੈ

I am used to getting up early. She is used to the cold. We are used to power cuts.

19. It depends on ...

यह ... पर निर्भर करता है · ਇਹ ... ਤੇ ਨਿਰਭਰ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ

It depends on the weather. It depends on the price. It depends on what my parents say.

20. I am sorry for ... / Thank you for ...

... के लिए माफ़ी / धन्यवाद · ... ਲਈ ਮਾਫ਼ੀ / ਧੰਨਵਾਦ

I am sorry for the delay. Thank you for your help. Thank you for coming.

Three things to notice

  • After to, the verb is in its first form: "I would like to go".
  • After at, about, for, like, used to (in pattern 18) and mind, the verb takes -ing: "good at cooking", "feel like sleeping", "used to getting up".
  • Pattern 5 ("I used to play", a past habit) and pattern 18 ("I am used to playing", a present habit you are comfortable with) look alike and mean different things.

How to practise

Take two patterns a day. For each, say the three examples, then make five of your own about your home, work or school. In ten days you will have used all twenty a hundred times. Then go to a situation page and notice the same patterns at work there.
