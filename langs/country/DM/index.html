How the languages of Dominica are spread

CLDR lists 1 language for Dominica. Only one of them, English, is used by at least a tenth of the people.

Official status

In the CLDR data, official: English. "Official" here is a simplified label: a country may have a national language, regional languages and working languages with different standing, and the law of each country has the final say.

Scripts you will see

The linked languages of Dominica are usually written in one script: Latin (English).

A taste of English

The most used language here with a page of its own is English (English). Its days of the week, from Monday, are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. A full date such as Saturday 14 November 2026 is written Saturday, November 14, 2026, and the number 1,234,567.89 is written 1,234,567.89. Open its page for the months and more.

Questions

What languages are spoken in Dominica?

CLDR lists 1: English.

Which language is used by most people in Dominica?

In the CLDR data it is English, at about 94% of the people.

Why do the shares not add up to 100%?

CLDR counts a person under every language they use, so shares can add up to more than 100%, and rough estimates can add up to less.

Percentages are CLDR's own estimates and are rough. How to read this data
