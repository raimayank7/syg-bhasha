Tamil language

Where it is used, its script, and its days, months, dates and numbers.

Tamil, called தமிழ் in the language itself, is written in the Tamil script, from left to right. CLDR lists it in 8 places. The highest share of the people of any one country is in Sri Lanka, where CLDR estimates that about 15% of the people use it (official). It is an official language of Sri Lanka, Singapore.

How dates and numbers are written

A full date for Saturday 14 November 2026 is written சனி, 14 நவம்பர், 2026, and the short form is 14/11/26. The number 1,234,567.89 is written 12,34,567.89.

Questions

Where is Tamil spoken?

CLDR lists Tamil in Sri Lanka, Réunion, India, Malaysia, United Kingdom, Mauritius and 2 more. The highest share of the people of any one country is in Sri Lanka (about 15%); the share says nothing about how many people that is.

Which script does Tamil use?

Tamil is written in the Tamil script.
