Kalenjin language

Where it is used, its script, and its days, months, dates and numbers.

Kalenjin, called Kalenjin in the language itself, is written in the Latin script, from left to right. CLDR lists it in 1 place. The highest share of the people of any one country is in Kenya, where CLDR estimates that about 7.6% of the people use it (not official).

How dates and numbers are written

A full date for Saturday 14 November 2026 is written Kolo, 14 Kipsuunde ne taai 2026, and the short form is 14/11/2026. The number 1,234,567.89 is written 1,234,567.89.
