Urdu language

Where it is used, its script, and its days, months, dates and numbers.

Urdu, called اردو in the language itself, is written in the Arabic script, from right to left. CLDR lists it in 5 places. The highest share of the people of any one country is in Pakistan, where CLDR estimates that about 95% of the people use it (official). It is an official language of Pakistan.

How dates and numbers are written

A full date for Saturday 14 November 2026 is written ہفتہ، 14 نومبر، 2026, and the short form is 14/11/26. The number 1,234,567.89 is written 1,234,567.89.

Notes on Urdu in this data

In the CLDR data for Urdu a week starts on پیر (Monday). The seven day names do not share an obvious ending or beginning, so each one is a separate word to learn. Among the twelve month names the longest is جولائی (6 letters) and the shortest is مئی (3 letters).

The number 1,234,567.89 is written 1,234,567.89 here. The mark between groups of digits is a comma and the decimal mark is a full stop, which matters when you read a price or a table in Urdu, because the same signs mean the opposite in other languages. The digits are grouped in threes.

Where it is listed

Urdu is listed in 5 places. It is official in 1 of them. The place where the smallest share of people uses it is Canada (about 1.1%), and the largest is Pakistan (about 95%). Shares come from CLDR and are rough, so use them to compare, not to count people.

Questions

Where is Urdu spoken?

CLDR lists Urdu in Pakistan, Mauritius, India, United Kingdom, Canada. The highest share of the people of any one country is in Pakistan (about 95%); the share says nothing about how many people that is.

Which script does Urdu use?

Urdu is written in the Arabic script.

Which day does the week start on in Urdu?

CLDR gives پیر (Monday) as the first day.

How do I write the number 1,234,567.89 in Urdu?

As 1,234,567.89 in CLDR's default style.
