French language

Where it is used, its script, and its days, months, dates and numbers.

French, called français in the language itself, is written in the Latin script, from left to right. CLDR lists it in 64 places. The highest share of the people of any one country is in St. Pierre & Miquelon, where CLDR estimates that about 100% of the people use it (official). It is an official language of St. Pierre & Miquelon, New Caledonia, French Polynesia, France, Monaco, Luxembourg, Réunion, St. Barthélemy and 35 more.

How dates and numbers are written

A full date for Saturday 14 November 2026 is written samedi 14 novembre 2026, and the short form is 14/11/2026. The number 1,234,567.89 is written 1 234 567,89.
