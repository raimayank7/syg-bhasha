Bangla language

Where it is used, its script, and its days, months, dates and numbers.

Bangla, called বাংলা in the language itself, is written in the Bangla script, from left to right. CLDR lists it in 5 places. The highest share of the people of any one country is in Bangladesh, where CLDR estimates that about 98% of the people use it (official). It is an official language of Bangladesh.

How dates and numbers are written

A full date for Saturday 14 November 2026 is written শনিবার, 14 নভেম্বর, 2026, and the short form is 14/11/26. The number 1,234,567.89 is written 12,34,567.89 (this language normally uses beng digits).

Questions

Where is Bangla spoken?

CLDR lists Bangla in Bangladesh, India, United Kingdom, Canada, Nepal. The highest share of the people of any one country is in Bangladesh (about 98%); the share says nothing about how many people that is.

Which script does Bangla use?

Bangla is written in the Bangla script.
