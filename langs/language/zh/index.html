Chinese language

Where it is used, its script, and its days, months, dates and numbers.

Chinese, called 中文 in the language itself, is written in the Simplified script, from left to right. CLDR lists it in 7 places. The highest share of the people of any one country is in China, where CLDR estimates that about 90% of the people use it (official). It is an official language of China, Singapore.

How dates and numbers are written

A full date for Saturday 14 November 2026 is written 2026年11月14日星期六, and the short form is 2026/11/14. The number 1,234,567.89 is written 1,234,567.89.

Questions

Where is Chinese spoken?

CLDR lists Chinese in China, Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong SAR China, Macao SAR China, Canada and 1 more. The highest share of the people of any one country is in China (about 90%); the share says nothing about how many people that is.

Which script does Chinese use?

Chinese is written in the Simplified script.
