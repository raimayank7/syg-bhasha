Tigre language

Where it is used, its script, and its days, months, dates and numbers.

Tigre, called ትግረ in the language itself, is written in the Ethiopic script, from left to right. CLDR lists it in 1 place. The highest share of the people of any one country is in Eritrea, where CLDR estimates that about 18% of the people use it (not official).

How dates and numbers are written

A full date for Saturday 14 November 2026 is written ሰንበት ንኢሽ፡ 14 ኖቬምበር ዮም 2026 ዓ/ም, and the short form is 14/11/26. The number 1,234,567.89 is written 1,234,567.89.

Notes on Tigre in this data

In the CLDR data for Tigre a week starts on ሰኖ (Monday). The seven day names do not share an obvious ending or beginning, so each one is a separate word to learn. Among the twelve month names the longest is ሴፕቴምበር (6 letters) and the shortest is ሜይ (2 letters).

The number 1,234,567.89 is written 1,234,567.89 here. The mark between groups of digits is a comma and the decimal mark is a full stop, which matters when you read a price or a table in Tigre, because the same signs mean the opposite in other languages. The digits are grouped in threes.

Where it is listed

Tigre is listed in 1 place. In none of them does CLDR give it official status. Shares come from CLDR and are rough, so use them to compare, not to count people.

Questions

Where is Tigre spoken?

CLDR lists Tigre in Eritrea. The highest share of the people of any one country is in Eritrea (about 18%); the share says nothing about how many people that is.

Which script does Tigre use?

Tigre is written in the Ethiopic script.

Which day does the week start on in Tigre?

CLDR gives ሰኖ (Monday) as the first day.

How do I write the number 1,234,567.89 in Tigre?

As 1,234,567.89 in CLDR's default style.
