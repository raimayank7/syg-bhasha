Rajasthani language

Where it is used, its script, and its days, months, dates and numbers.

Rajasthani, called राजस्थानी in the language itself, is written in the Devanagari script, from left to right. CLDR lists it in 1 place. The highest share of the people of any one country is in India, where CLDR estimates that about 0.1% of the people use it (not official).

How dates and numbers are written

A full date for Saturday 14 November 2026 is written 2026 नवंबर 14, शनिवार, and the short form is 2026-11-14. The number 1,234,567.89 is written 1,234,567.89 (this language normally uses deva digits).

Notes on Rajasthani in this data

In the CLDR data for Rajasthani a week starts on सोमवार (Monday). All seven day names end with वार, so learning that one ending covers part of every day name. Among the twelve month names the longest is सितम्बर (7 letters) and the shortest is मई (2 letters).

The number 1,234,567.89 is written 1,234,567.89 here. The mark between groups of digits is a comma and the decimal mark is a full stop, which matters when you read a price or a table in Rajasthani, because the same signs mean the opposite in other languages. The digits are grouped in threes.

Where it is listed

Rajasthani is listed in 1 place. In none of them does CLDR give it official status. Shares come from CLDR and are rough, so use them to compare, not to count people.

Questions

Where is Rajasthani spoken?

CLDR lists Rajasthani in India. The highest share of the people of any one country is in India (about 0.1%); the share says nothing about how many people that is.

Which script does Rajasthani use?

Rajasthani is written in the Devanagari script.

Which day does the week start on in Rajasthani?

CLDR gives सोमवार (Monday) as the first day.

How do I write the number 1,234,567.89 in Rajasthani?

As 1,234,567.89 in CLDR's default style.

Which languages use the same script as Rajasthani?

It is written in the Devanagari script; open the script page to see the others.
