A leopard cannot change its spots

People do not change their basic nature.

What it means

People do not change their basic nature.

An example

He will not stop boasting; a leopard cannot change its spots.

When to use it

Use it when the situation matches this idea: people do not change their basic nature. Say it lightly and only once in a conversation or a piece of writing: read using proverbs well for more. It has 6 words.
