There is no place like home

What it means

Home is the most comfortable place of all.

An example

After the long trip, there is no place like home.

When to use it

Use it when the situation matches this idea: home is the most comfortable place of all. Say it lightly and only once in a conversation or a piece of writing: read using proverbs well for more. It has 6 words.
