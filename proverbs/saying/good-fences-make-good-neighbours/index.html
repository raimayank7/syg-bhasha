Good fences make good neighbours

Clear limits between people keep the peace.

What it means

Clear limits between people keep the peace.

An example

We agreed where the wall stands, because good fences make good neighbours.

When to use it

Use it when the situation matches this idea: clear limits between people keep the peace. Say it lightly and only once in a conversation or a piece of writing: read using proverbs well for more. It has 5 words.
