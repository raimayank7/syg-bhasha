Honesty is the best policy

It is wiser and better to tell the truth.

What it means

It is wiser and better to tell the truth.

An example

Admit the mistake now; honesty is the best policy.

When to use it

Use it when the situation matches this idea: it is wiser and better to tell the truth. Say it lightly and only once in a conversation or a piece of writing: read using proverbs well for more. It has 5 words.

How it sounds

Speakers usually say it as advice. It is only 5 words long, so it is quick to say and hard to forget. Its longest word is "honesty" (7 letters).

The picture inside it

Most proverbs put an idea into a small scene. Here the scene is a plain, straight path compared with a twisting one. When you meet the saying, picture that scene and then ask how the situation in front of you is like it.

Another example

He admitted the mistake at once; honesty is the best policy.

Try it yourself

Write one sentence of your own that ends with "honesty is the best policy", about something that really happened to you or a person you know. If the meaning, "it is wiser and better to tell the truth", fits your story, you have understood the proverb.
