Learn to read Bangla in ten days: a plan for Hindi readers

The Bengali script looks unfamiliar for about three days. Then a Hindi reader begins to see through it, because underneath it is the same system: the same order of letters, the same matras, the same idea of conjuncts. This plan uses that kinship at every step. It asks for about twenty minutes a day.

The ten days

Why Hindi readers learn fast

  • Same order. Recite the Hindi varnamala and you have the Bengali one. Only the names change from "ka" to "ko".
  • Same matras. Every kar has a Hindi twin, and the i-kar even sits on the left as ि does.
  • Shared words. Thousands of Bangla words are Sanskrit words you already know. You can check your own reading against your Hindi.
  • Some shapes are alike. গ ম ন ণ প ষ are near relations of ग म न ण प ष.

Where you will stumble

  • Signs on the left. In কে and কো the vowel sign stands before the consonant. Train your eye to take in the whole cluster before sounding it.
  • Look-alike letters. ব and র, খ and থ, ড and ড়, য and য়. A dot makes all the difference.
  • Conjuncts. Accept that these come slowly, one word at a time.
  • Reading it as Hindi. You will read সত্য correctly as letters and say "satya". The Bangla is "shotto". Reading the letters and saying them the Bengali way are two skills; the second comes from listening. See Bangla pronunciation.

Daily method

  1. Five minutes: revise yesterday's letters by writing them from memory.
  2. Ten minutes: learn today's letters, writing each beside its Devanagari twin and saying both aloud.
  3. Five minutes: read today's words, then find today's letters on any Bangla page, such as a news site, even if you cannot yet read the words around them.

After day ten

Read a little real Bangla every day: headlines, shop signs in a Bengali neighbourhood, the Bangla line on a banknote, the lyrics of a Rabindrasangeet you like to hear. Songs are especially good, because the tune tells you how the words are said. Keep a notebook with new conjuncts and new words. In a couple of months the letters stop being a puzzle and become simply writing.

This plan teaches the script. Because Bangla is a different language from Hindi, with its own verbs and endings, you will also want the phrases and the short grammar guide.
