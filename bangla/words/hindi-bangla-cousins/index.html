Hindi words and their Bangla cousins

24 words in Bangla, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Bangla voice.

Bangla and Hindi are sister languages. Both descend from Sanskrit through the Prakrits, and they share thousands of words. A Hindi speaker reading a Bangla sentence aloud often recognises word after word, once four regular sound changes are understood.

  1. The inherent "a" becomes "o". Every consonant in Hindi carries a short अ; in Bangla it carries a short rounded "o". जल is jol, मन is mon.
  2. All three s-letters are said "sh". सुंदर is shundor, सात is shat.
  3. "v" becomes "b". Bangla has one letter, ব, for both. विजय is bijoy, वन is bon.
  4. "y" at the start of a word becomes "j". यमुना is jomuna, योग is jog.

Conjuncts that are simplified in speech

Bangla keeps the Sanskrit spelling of many words but says them more simply. ক্ষ (ksh) is said "kkh" or "kh": लक्ष्मी becomes lokkhi, क्षमा becomes khoma. A য or ব joined under a consonant is usually not said; it doubles the consonant instead: सत्य is shotto, विद्या is biddya, विश्व is bissho. The page on conjuncts lists the common ones.

False friends

  • দাদা (dada) is an elder brother in Bangla, a grandfather in Hindi.
  • কাকা (kaka) is father's younger brother, and মামা (mama) is mother's brother, as in Hindi; but দাদু (dadu) is a grandfather.
  • খাওয়া (khaoa), "to eat", is also used for drinking: জল খাওয়া, "to eat water".
  • চুল (chul) is hair; রাগ (rag) is anger as well as a musical raga.
