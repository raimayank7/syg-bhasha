Half the words are already yours

A large part of the Bangla vocabulary comes from Sanskrit, as Hindi vocabulary does. Often the word is the same and only the sound has shifted: सुंदर is shundor, समय is shomoy, विजय is bijoy. The page of Hindi and Bangla cousin words sets out the four regular sound shifts, and once you have them you will recognise words all over the other lists.

The lists

  • Numbers, with the Bengali digits that are used on every price tag and bus.
  • Family, including the famous dada and didi, and the false friends for Hindi speakers.
  • Days and time, with the Bengali months.
  • Food words, from bhat to roshogolla.
  • Common verbs, each in the "I" form with the pattern for the other persons.

How to learn from a list

  1. Read the Bangla first and try to say it before looking at the roman line.
  2. Notice which words you can link to Hindi and which are truly new; spend your effort on the new ones.
  3. Put each word in a short sentence with আছে (there is), দিন (please give) or ভালো লাগে (I like).
  4. Use the Words mode of the practice quiz to test yourself.
