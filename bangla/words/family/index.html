Family

21 words in Bangla, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Bangla voice.

Bangla family words are as precise as Hindi ones, but several of them will trip up a Hindi speaker, because the same word means something different. The famous one is দাদা (dada). In Hindi, दादा is your father's father. In Bangla, dada is your elder brother, and by extension any man a little older than you, shortened to "-da" after a name: Rahul-da. A grandfather is ঠাকুরদা (thakurda) on the father's side and দাদু (dadu) on the mother's side, though usage varies between families and between West Bengal and Bangladesh.

The elder sister is দিদি (didi), as in Hindi, shortened to "-di" after a name. Younger ones are ভাই (bhai) and বোন (bon).

Uncles and aunts are sorted by side, as in Hindi but with different words. Father's younger brother is কাকা (kaka), father's elder brother জ্যাঠা (jyatha), father's sister পিসি (pishi), mother's brother মামা (mama) and mother's sister মাসি (mashi). Their wives and husbands each have a matching word.

Notes

  • Because -দা (-da) and -দি (-di) are added to names as a mark of friendly respect, you will hear them everywhere in Bengal: at work, in shops and for well-known people. They are the Bangla answer to the Hindi भैया and दीदी.
  • Muslim Bengali families commonly use a different set: আব্বা (abba) father, আম্মা (amma) mother, চাচা (chacha), খালা (khala), ফুফু (phuphu), নানা (nana).
  • স্বামী is spelt "swami" but said "shami"; the ব joined to the s is silent. See Bangla pronunciation.
