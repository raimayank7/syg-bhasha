Days and time

20 words in Bangla, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Bangla voice.

The Bangla days of the week are the Hindi days in Bengali dress. Each is the name of the same heavenly body followed by বার (bar), the Hindi वार: सोमवार is সোমবার, said "shombar"; मंगलवार is মঙ্গলবার, "mongolbar". Only the sounds shift: s becomes sh, a becomes o, v becomes b. If you learn those three shifts here, you have learnt them for the whole language.

Thursday is the long one: বৃহস্পতিবার (brihoshpotibar), the day of Brihaspati, where Hindi usually says गुरुवार and sometimes बृहस्पतिवार. Sunday is রবিবার (robibar), from रवि, the sun.

The day is divided more finely than in Hindi. Between দুপুর (dupur, noon and early afternoon) and সন্ধ্যা (shondhya, dusk) comes বিকেল (bikel), the late afternoon, the time for tea and a walk. As in Hindi, one word serves for both yesterday and tomorrow: কাল (kal); to be exact, people say গতকাল (gotokal) for yesterday and আগামীকাল (agamikal) for tomorrow.

The Bengali months and seasons

Bengal counts six seasons of two months each, and the Bengali calendar year begins in mid-April with বৈশাখ (Boishakh). The twelve months are বৈশাখ, জ্যৈষ্ঠ, আষাঢ়, শ্রাবণ, ভাদ্র, আশ্বিন, কার্তিক, অগ্রহায়ণ, পৌষ, মাঘ, ফাল্গুন, চৈত্র: Boishakh, Joishtho, Asharh, Shrabon, Bhadro, Ashshin, Kartik, Ogrohayon, Poush, Magh, Phalgun, Choitro. You will recognise the Hindi वैशाख, ज्येष्ठ, आषाढ़, श्रावण in them.

For official and daily business the common calendar is used, with the English month names written in Bangla: জানুয়ারি, ফেব্রুয়ারি, মার্চ.
