Food words

23 words in Bangla, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Bangla voice.

Food words are where a learner of Bangla feels the kinship with Hindi most strongly, and also meets the first surprises. ডাল (dal), দুধ (dudh), চা (cha) and আম (am) need no explaining. But the roti of North India is রুটি (ruti), cooked rice is ভাত (bhat), and uncooked rice is a different word, চাল (chal), exactly as Hindi separates भात and चावल.

Vegetables have some of the most different names. Potato is the familiar আলু (alu), but onion is পেঁয়াজ (peyaj), brinjal is বেগুন (begun), and pumpkin is কুমড়ো (kumro). A cooked vegetable dish is a তরকারি (torkari), the Hindi तरकारी.

Then there are the sweets, made mostly from ছানা (chhana), fresh curdled-milk cheese. Their names have travelled across India: রসগোল্লা, সন্দেশ, মিষ্টি দই. Note how Bengalis say them: roshogolla, shondesh, mishti doi.

Notes

  • সন্দেশ also means "news" or "message", as संदेश does in Hindi. The sweet shares the word.
  • Mustard is at the heart of Bengali cooking: সর্ষে (shorshe) is mustard, and সর্ষের তেল (shorsher tel) is mustard oil.
  • To say you like something: আমার মিষ্টি ভালো লাগে (amar mishti bhalo lage), "I like sweets". To ask for it: add দিন (din), "please give".
