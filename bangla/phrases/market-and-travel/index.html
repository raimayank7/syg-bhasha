Market and travel

15 phrases in Bangla, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Bangla voice.

Two question words open most doors in a new city. কত? (koto?) is "how much?", and কোথায়? (kothay?) is "where?". Point at a thing and ask এটার দাম কত? (etar dam koto?), "what is the price of this?" Name a place and ask ... কোথায়?, "where is ...?"

Bangla has a handy habit of adding -টা (-ta) to a noun to mean "the" or "this particular one": বইটা (boita) the book, এটা (eta) this one, ওটা (ota) that one. Hindi has nothing quite like it; the nearest is "वाला" in "यह वाला". You will hear -টা in almost every sentence in a market.

Directions are easy for a Hindi speaker, since the words are cousins: ডান (dan) right, বাঁ (ban) left, সোজা (shoja) straight, কাছে (kachhe) near, দূরে (dure) far. In Kolkata you will use them for the yellow taxis, the metro, the trams and the buses whose conductors call out the route.

  • এটার দাম কত?

    etar dam koto?

    इसका दाम कितना है?

    What is the price of this?

  • খুব বেশি দাম

    khub beshi dam

    बहुत ज़्यादा दाम है

    The price is too high

  • একটু কম করুন

    ektu kom korun

    थोड़ा कम कीजिए

    Please reduce it a little

  • আমি এটা নেব

    ami eta nebo

    मैं यह लूँगा

    I will take this

  • অন্য রং আছে?

    onno rong achhe?

    कोई और रंग है?

    Do you have another colour?

  • স্টেশন কোথায়?

    steshon kothay?

    स्टेशन कहाँ है?

    Where is the station?

  • এখান থেকে কত দূর?

    ekhan theke koto dur?

    यहाँ से कितनी दूर है?

    How far is it from here?

  • সোজা যান

    shoja jan

    सीधे जाइए

    Go straight

  • ডান দিকে যান

    dan dike jan

    दाईं ओर जाइए

    Go to the right

  • বাঁ দিকে যান

    ban dike jan

    बाईं ओर जाइए

    Go to the left

  • এখানে থামুন

    ekhane thamun

    यहाँ रुकिए

    Please stop here

  • হাওড়া যাবেন?

    haora jaben?

    हावड़ा चलेंगे?

    Will you go to Howrah?

  • ভাড়া কত?

    bhara koto?

    किराया कितना है?

    What is the fare?

  • এই বাস কোথায় যাবে?

    ei bas kothay jabe?

    यह बस कहाँ जाएगी?

    Where does this bus go?

  • আমি রাস্তা চিনি না

    ami rasta chini na

    मुझे रास्ता नहीं पता

    I do not know the way

Notes

  • যান (jan) is "please go", from যাওয়া (jaoa), to go. The letter য looks like the Hindi य but is said "j".
  • আছে (achhe) means "there is" or "have": আছে? "Do you have it?" The answer is আছে (yes, there is) or নেই (nei, there is not).
  • Useful place words: বাজার bajar (market), দোকান dokan (shop), হাসপাতাল hashpatal (hospital), বিমানবন্দর bimanbondor (airport), রাস্তা rasta (road), মোড় mor (crossing).
