How the phrase pages work

Each page opens with a short note on the one or two points of language that matter most for that situation, then gives the phrases as cards. The Bangla comes first, in its own script. Below it are a roman reading that follows the real sound, the meaning in Hindi and the meaning in English. Tap Listen to hear the phrase where your device has a Bangla voice, and Copy to keep or send it.

The phrases are in polite standard Bangla, using আপনি (apni), the respectful "you", unless a card says otherwise. That is the safe choice with strangers and elders. With friends, Bengalis use তুমি (tumi), and the verb ending changes with it, as the greetings page shows.

Your Hindi is a help

Bangla and Hindi are close relatives. Sentence order is the same, so the Hindi line under each phrase usually matches the Bangla almost word for word. Many words are the same too, once you allow for the Bengali sound: নাম nam, ভালো bhalo, ধন্যবাদ dhonnobad. What is different is the verbs and the endings, and those are what the phrase pages quietly teach.

Where to begin

Start with greetings and yes, no, please and thank you. If you are visiting Kolkata or have Bengali friends and neighbours, food and festival greetings will earn you the warmest response. Say each phrase aloud several times; Bangla is a musical language and is learnt by the ear.
