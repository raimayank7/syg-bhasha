Feelings

15 phrases in Bangla, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Bangla voice.

The Bangla word every outsider knows is ভালোবাসি (bhalobashi), "I love". It is built from ভালো (bhalo), "good", which is the busiest word in the language. ভালো আছি is "I am well". ভালো লাগছে (bhalo lagchhe) is "it feels good; I am liking it". ভালো লাগে না is "I do not like it". And আমি তোমাকে ভালোবাসি (ami tomake bhalobashi) is "I love you".

As in Hindi, many feelings are things that "happen to" a person. Hindi says "मुझे डर लग रहा है"; Bangla says আমার ভয় করছে (amar bhoy korchhe), "my fear is doing". The person takes আমার (amar, "my") instead of "I". Once you see that amar often plays the part of मुझे, these sentences fall into place.

A word with no good translation is আড্ডা (adda): long, unhurried, happy talk among friends over tea. If a Bengali friend says চলো, আড্ডা দিই, clear your evening.

    Congratulations

Notes

  • মন (mon) is the mind and heart together, the Hindi मन. মন খারাপ (mon kharap), "the mind is spoilt", is the everyday way to say one is sad.
  • মনে পড়া (mone pora), "to fall into the mind", is to remember or to miss someone.
  • খুব (khub) is "very", the same word as ख़ूब. দারুণ (darun) is the favourite Bengali word of praise.
  • To a person you address as apni, "I love/respect you" changes to আপনাকে (apnake): the ending -কে (-ke) is the Hindi को.
