How the quiz works

In Letters mode you see one Bengali letter and choose its roman sound from four options. The vowels and consonants from our alphabet lessons are all included. In Words mode you see a whole word, with its vowel signs and conjuncts, and choose its English meaning; the words come from our word lists. After each answer the correct option turns green and the full reading is shown. Your score is kept only while the page is open. Nothing is stored or sent.

A note on the sounds

The quiz uses the real Bangla sound of each letter, so য is "j" and শ is "sh". Letters that share a sound, such as ই and ঈ, never appear together among the options, so there is always exactly one right answer.

How to practise well

  1. Say the Hindi twin as soon as the letter appears: see ক, think क, say "ko".
  2. Watch the look-alikes: ব and র, খ and থ, ঘ and ষ.
  3. Move to Words early. Reading whole words is the real skill, and many of the words are ones you can guess from Hindi.
  4. Copy out what you miss, five times each.
  5. Little and often. Three rounds of two minutes spread through the day work better than one long sitting.

After the quiz

When the letters come easily, go to the phrase pages, cover the roman line and read the Bangla aloud. Then read something real: a headline, a shop sign, the Bangla on a banknote. Each word you work out on your own is worth ten in a quiz.
