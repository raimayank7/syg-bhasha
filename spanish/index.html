Questions

Why is my verb missing?

Only 47 common verbs are covered: regular verbs and 14 irregular ones. Stem-changing verbs such as pensar or dormir are not included yet.

Are the forms checked?

Yes. Each form was compared with the Wiktionary conjugation tables, tense by tense. See About.
