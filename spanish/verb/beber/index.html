beber: conjugation

to drink

beber means to drink. It is an -er verb, and it is a regular verb: its endings follow the pattern of all verbs that end in -er. The gerund is bebiendo and the past participle is bebido.

To conjugate it, remove -er to get the stem beb and add the ending for the tense and person; in the future and conditional the whole infinitive is the base. The forms below were checked against Wiktionary.
