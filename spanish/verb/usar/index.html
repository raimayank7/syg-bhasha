usar: conjugation

to use

usar means to use. It is an -ar verb, and it is a regular verb: its endings follow the pattern of all verbs that end in -ar. The gerund is usando and the past participle is usado.

To conjugate it, remove -ar to get the stem us and add the ending for the tense and person; in the future and conditional the whole infinitive is the base. The forms below were checked against Wiktionary.

Related verbs
