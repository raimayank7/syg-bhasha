ayudar: conjugation

to help

ayudar means to help. It is an -ar verb, and it is a regular verb: its endings follow the pattern of all verbs that end in -ar. The gerund is ayudando and the past participle is ayudado.

To conjugate it, remove -ar to get the stem ayud and add the ending for the tense and person; in the future and conditional the whole infinitive is the base. The forms below were checked against Wiktionary.
