Barakhadi quiz

Four choices, one right answer. Pick a letter row or mix them all. Nothing is timed.

0 right out of 0

How to play

A form from the barakhadi appears in the box. Tap the answer that matches it. A right answer turns green. A wrong answer shows which one was right, and the next question follows by itself. The score at the bottom counts right answers out of all questions; it is not saved, so every visit starts fresh.

Three kinds of question

Read. A Hindi form such as गू is shown and the choices are English spellings. This checks that the child can tell the matras apart. Spell. The English spelling is shown, for example "goo", and the choices are Hindi forms. This is the harder direction and is good practice before writing. Hear. The device says a form aloud and the choices are Hindi forms. This needs a Hindi voice on the phone or computer; if the sound is missing or odd, use the first two kinds.

Using the quiz well

  • Start with one letter row that the child has just learnt, not with all letters mixed.
  • Ten questions are enough for one sitting. Stop while the child is still winning.
  • Wrong answers are useful. Many of the choices are forms of the same letter with a different matra, so a mistake shows exactly which two signs are being mixed, such as कि and की. Open that matra page together afterwards.
  • Let the child read the right answer aloud each time. Saying it fixes it better than tapping.

The spellings follow the chart on barakhadi in English; hard ट ठ ड ढ are shown with capital letters. For written practice, print a sheet from practice sheets.
