How to read the chart

Each row is one consonant. Each column is one vowel sign, in the order of the Hindi vowels: अ आ इ ई उ ऊ ए ऐ ओ औ अं अः. Read a row from left to right in the rhythm children learn at school, four forms at a time: क का कि की, कु कू के कै, को कौ कं कः. The letter at the start of a row opens that letter's own page, and the vowel at the top of a column opens the page for that matra.

The chart has thirty-three consonants and the three joined letters क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ. On a phone, swipe the table sideways; the first column stays in place so you always know which row you are in.

What is barakhadi?

In Hindi, a vowel that follows a consonant is not written as a separate letter. It becomes a small sign, called a matra, attached to the consonant: before it, after it, above it or below it. The barakhadi is the table that shows every consonant with every one of these signs. The name means "twelve forms". A child who knows the alphabet but not the barakhadi can name letters but cannot read words, which is why schools teach it straight after the alphabet. More in what is barakhadi.

Learn it step by step

  1. First words. Read words without any matra, such as घर and कमल.
  2. One sign at a time. Take the matras in order, starting with आ की मात्रा.
  3. One row a day. Open a letter page, hear the row, read its words and sentences.
  4. Write. Print a practice sheet for the same letter.
  5. Check. Play the quiz for that row.

For parents and teachers

The guide how to teach barakhadi at home gives a daily routine that takes ten minutes. Common mistakes lists the errors nearly every learner makes, such as mixing घ and ध or रु and रू, and how to fix each. After the twelve forms, go on to other signs (ऋ, chandrabindu, nukta) and half letters.

Questions

Why are some boxes rarely used?

The chart is complete on purpose, but Hindi does not use every form. No word begins with ङ, ञ or ण, and forms such as यि or वू hardly occur. The letter pages say honestly which forms have everyday words.

Is the sound correct when I tap a form?

The sound comes from the Hindi voice of your own device. Most phones say the syllables well; a few read single forms oddly or have no Hindi voice. Treat it as a help and let a Hindi speaker confirm when in doubt.

Can I print the chart?

Yes. Use the Print button above. Choose landscape in the print window so that all twelve columns fit on one A4 page.
