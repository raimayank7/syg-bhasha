Barakhadi

Half letters and joined letters

When two consonants come together with no vowel between them, Hindi joins them into one shape. The first consonant becomes a half letter and leans on the second. This is the last big step after the barakhadi, and it follows a small number of patterns.

Pattern 1: letters with a stick lose the stick

Most Hindi letters end in a vertical stick on the right: ग, च, त, न, प, म, स and many more. To make the half form, drop the stick and attach what is left to the next letter:

  • स् + त = स्त, as in रास्ता (road), बस्ता (school bag)
  • प् + य = प्य, as in प्यार (love), प्यास (thirst)
  • न् + द = न्द, as in आनन्द (joy)
  • च् + छ = च्छ, as in अच्छा (good)
  • ल् + ल = ल्ल, as in बिल्ली (cat)

Pattern 2: क and फ lose their curl

क and फ have a stick in the middle and a curl on the right. Their half forms cut the curl short: क्या (what), मक्खी (fly), हफ़्ता (week).

Pattern 3: round letters use the halant or stack

ट, ठ, ड, ढ, द and ह have no stick to drop. In older print the second letter is stacked below the first: ट्ट in छुट्टी, द्द in गद्दा. In modern print you will often see the halant stroke instead. Both are correct. द has several fixed joins worth knowing by sight: द्व (द्वार, door), द्ध (शुद्ध, pure), द्य (विद्या, learning).

The four shapes of र

र is the letter that changes most, and it has its own rules:

  1. र first, as a hook on top (reph). When र comes before another consonant with no vowel, it is written as a small hook above the next letter: कर्म (deed), धर्म, सूर्य, पर्वत. The hook is written over the letter that is spoken after it.
  2. र second, as a slanting stroke. After a letter with a stick, र becomes a small stroke from the stick: क्रम (order), प्रेम (love), ग्राम (village), भ्रम.
  3. र second, as a caret below. After round letters it looks like a small upturned V: ट्रक (truck), ड्रम, राष्ट्र.
  4. Special shapes. त + र gives त्र, and श + र gives श्र, as in श्री and श्रम.

The joined letters taught with the alphabet

Four joins are so common that schools teach them as letters:

  • क्ष = क् + ष: क्षमा (forgiveness), कक्षा (class), पक्षी (bird). See क्ष की बारहखड़ी.
  • त्र = त् + र: पत्र (letter), मित्र (friend), चित्र (picture). See त्र की बारहखड़ी.
  • ज्ञ = ज् + ञ, said "gya": ज्ञान (knowledge), विज्ञान (science). See ज्ञ की बारहखड़ी.
  • श्र = श् + र: श्रम (labour), श्रीमान (mister), विश्राम (rest).

Matras on joined letters

A matra belongs to the whole join, not to its second half. In स्कूल the ू hangs below the joined स्क. The short इ sign stands before the whole cluster, which surprises learners: in स्थिति the flag of ि comes before स्थ, and in शक्ति it comes before क्त.

Doubled letters

Hindi doubles consonants often, and the doubling is spoken: the sound is held a little longer. पक्का (firm), बच्चा (child), अम्मा (mother), पत्ता (leaf), गन्ना (sugarcane). Compare पता (address) with पत्ता (leaf).

How to practise

Take one pattern a week. Read ten words, then write five. Start with doubled letters, because the child already knows both halves. Leave the reph of र for last. Until these joins are easy, keep reading simple words from the letter pages so that confidence stays high.
