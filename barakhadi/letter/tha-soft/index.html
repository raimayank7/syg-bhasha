About the letter थ

थ belongs to the group त वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is the soft "t" of त with a puff of breath. The tip of the tongue touches the back of the upper front teeth. This row is called दंत्य, and its sounds are softer than their English cousins.

It is the second letter of its row. The mouth does exactly what it does for त, and then lets out a clear puff of breath. Hold a thin strip of paper before your lips: it should jump for थ and stay still for त.

The twelve forms with words

Tap a form to hear it. Under each form you see how it is written in English letters, the name of its matra and an example word.

  • thiछोटी इ की मात्राथिरकनाto dance lightly
  • theeबड़ी ई की मात्राथीwas
  • thuछोटे उ की मात्राno everyday word
  • thooबड़े ऊ की मात्राno everyday word
  • theए की मात्राno everyday word
  • thaiऐ की मात्राथैलाbag
  • thoओ की मात्राथोड़ाa little
  • thauऔ की मात्राno everyday word
  • thanअनुस्वार (बिंदी)no everyday word
  • thahविसर्गno everyday word

Only 6 of the twelve forms of थ appear in everyday words; the list above shows them, and in some of them the form comes in the middle or at the end of the word rather than at the start. The remaining forms are for chart practice only.

Watch out

थ and य look alike. थ begins with a small loop at the top left; य does not. थ is also not the "th" of English "the": it is a breathy soft t.

Short sentences to read

  • थाली में थोड़ा खाना है।
    There is a little food on the plate.
  • थैला भारी है।
    The bag is heavy.

Questions

How do you write थ barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of थ are: tha, thaa, thi, thee, thu, thoo, the, thai, tho, thau, than, thah.

What sound does थ make?

The sound of थ is the soft "t" of त with a puff of breath.
