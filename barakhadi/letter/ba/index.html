ब की बारहखड़ी (Ba barakhadi)

About the letter ब

ब belongs to the group प वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is like the "b" in "bat". Both lips close and then open. This row is called ओष्ठ्य. You can see the sound being made in a mirror.

It is the third letter of its row, the voiced one. Put two fingers on your throat: you feel a hum for ब and none for प. There is no extra breath; the breathy form of this sound is भ.

The twelve forms with words

Tap a form to hear it. Under each form you see how it is written in English letters, the name of its matra and an example word.

11 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

ब and व differ by one small slanting line inside the belly. ब has the line, व does not. बन (become) and वन (forest) are different words.

Short sentences to read

  • बंदर बाग में बैठा है।
    The monkey is sitting in the garden.
  • बादल बरस रहे हैं।
    The clouds are raining.

Questions

How do you write ब barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of ब are: ba, baa, bi, bee, bu, boo, be, bai, bo, bau, ban, bah.

What sound does ब make?

The sound of ब is like the "b" in "bat".
