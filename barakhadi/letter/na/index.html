About the letter न

न belongs to the group त वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is like the "n" in "nut", with the tongue on the teeth. The tip of the tongue touches the back of the upper front teeth. This row is called दंत्य, and its sounds are softer than their English cousins.

It is the fifth letter of its row, the nasal: the mouth takes the same position as for त, but the air goes out through the nose. Pinch your nose while saying it and the sound stops.

The twelve forms with words

Tap a form to hear it. Under each form you see how it is written in English letters, the name of its matra and an example word.

  • naअ (कोई मात्रा नहीं)नलtap
  • naaआ की मात्रानाकnose
  • niछोटी इ की मात्रानिशानmark
  • neeबड़ी ई की मात्रानीलाblue
  • nuछोटे उ की मात्रानुकसानloss
  • nooबड़े ऊ की मात्रानूपुरanklet
  • neए की मात्रानेताleader
  • naiऐ की मात्रानैयाboat
  • noओ की मात्रानोटnote
  • nauऔ की मात्रानौnine
  • nanअनुस्वार (बिंदी)नंबरnumber
  • nahविसर्गपुनःagain

12 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

न is the everyday "n" of Hindi. Of the three other nasal letters ङ, ञ and ण, only ण is still written often, and only inside words.

Short sentences to read

  • नानी नल से पानी भरती हैं।
    Nani fills water from the tap.
  • नाव नदी में है।
    The boat is in the river.

Questions

How do you write न barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of न are: na, naa, ni, nee, nu, noo, ne, nai, no, nau, nan, nah.

What sound does न make?

The sound of न is like the "n" in "nut", with the tongue on the teeth.
