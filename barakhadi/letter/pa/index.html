About the letter प

प belongs to the group प वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is like the "p" in "spin": no puff of air. Both lips close and then open. This row is called ओष्ठ्य. You can see the sound being made in a mirror.

It is the first letter of its row, the plain one: the throat does not hum and no puff of breath follows. Hold your palm in front of your mouth and say प: you should feel almost no air. Its breathy partner is फ, and its voiced partner is ब.

The twelve forms with words

  • pauऔ की मात्रापौधाplant

11 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

प, ष and य share the same stick-and-belly shape. प is open at the top. ष has a slanting line inside. य has a hook at the left.

Short sentences to read

  • पापा पतंग लाए।
    Papa brought a kite.
  • पेड़ पर पीला पत्ता है।
    There is a yellow leaf on the tree.

Questions

How do you write प barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of प are: pa, paa, pi, pee, pu, poo, pe, pai, po, pau, pan, pah.

What sound does प make?

The sound of प is like the "p" in "spin": no puff of air.
