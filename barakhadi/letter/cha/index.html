About the letter च

च belongs to the group च वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is like the "ch" in "church" but lighter, with no puff of air. The flat middle of the tongue presses against the hard palate, just behind the ridge of the upper teeth. This row is called तालव्य.

It is the first letter of its row, the plain one: the throat does not hum and no puff of breath follows. Hold your palm in front of your mouth and say च: you should feel almost no air. Its breathy partner is छ, and its voiced partner is ज.

11 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

In Roman letters च is written "ch" and छ is written "chh". Many children write "cha" for both. Say them aloud: छ has a strong breath.

Short sentences to read

  • चाचा चाय पी रहे हैं।
    Uncle is drinking tea.
  • चिड़िया चावल चुगती है।
    The bird pecks at rice.

Questions

How do you write च barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of च are: cha, chaa, chi, chee, chu, choo, che, chai, cho, chau, chan, chah.

What sound does च make?

The sound of च is like the "ch" in "church" but lighter, with no puff of air.
