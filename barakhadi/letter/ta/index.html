About the letter ट

ट belongs to the group ट वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is a hard "t" with the tongue curled back, close to the "t" in English "top" as Indians say it. The tip of the tongue curls backwards and strikes the roof of the mouth. This row is called मूर्धन्य, and it gives Hindi its typical hard t and d sounds.

It is the first letter of its row, the plain one: the throat does not hum and no puff of breath follows. Hold your palm in front of your mouth and say ट: you should feel almost no air. Its breathy partner is ठ, and its voiced partner is ड.

10 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

ट (hard, tongue curled back) and त (soft, tongue on the teeth) are different letters. English "t" sounds like ट to a Hindi speaker: टिकट, टमाटर.

Short sentences to read

  • टोपी पहनो।
    Wear a cap.

Questions

How do you write ट barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of ट are: Ta, Taa, Ti, Tee, Tu, Too, Te, Tai, To, Tau, Tan, Tah.

What sound does ट make?

The sound of ट is a hard "t" with the tongue curled back, close to the "t" in English "top" as Indians say it.
