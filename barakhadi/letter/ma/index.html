म की बारहखड़ी (Ma barakhadi)

About the letter म

म belongs to the group प वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is like the "m" in "man". Both lips close and then open. This row is called ओष्ठ्य. You can see the sound being made in a mirror.

It is the fifth letter of its row, the nasal: the mouth takes the same position as for प, but the air goes out through the nose. Pinch your nose while saying it and the sound stops.

The twelve forms with words

Tap a form to hear it. Under each form you see how it is written in English letters, the name of its matra and an example word.

  • maअ (कोई मात्रा नहीं)मछलीfish
  • maaआ की मात्रामालाgarland
  • miछोटी इ की मात्रामिठाईsweets
  • meeबड़ी ई की मात्रामीठाsweet
  • muछोटे उ की मात्रामुर्गाrooster
  • mooबड़े ऊ की मात्रामूलीradish
  • meए की मात्रामेलाfair
  • maiऐ की मात्रामैदानopen ground
  • moओ की मात्रामोरpeacock
  • mauऔ की मात्रामौसमweather
  • manअनुस्वार (बिंदी)मंदिरtemple
  • mahविसर्गनमःsalutation

12 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

म and भ: look at the top line. Full line means म. Broken line with a curl means भ.

Short sentences to read

  • माँ मिठाई लाई।
    Mother brought sweets.
  • मोर मैदान में नाचा।
    The peacock danced in the open ground.

Questions

How do you write म barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of म are: ma, maa, mi, mee, mu, moo, me, mai, mo, mau, man, mah.

What sound does म make?

The sound of म is like the "m" in "man".
