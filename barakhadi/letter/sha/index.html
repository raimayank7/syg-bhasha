About the letter श

श belongs to the group ऊष्म of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is like the "sh" in "ship". The flat middle of the tongue presses against the hard palate, just behind the ridge of the upper teeth. This row is called तालव्य.

It belongs to the four letters श ष स ह that grammar books call ऊष्म, the "warm" letters, because breath rubs through a narrow gap as they are said. The sound can be held for as long as the breath lasts.

  • shauऔ की मात्राशौकhobby

11 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

Hindi has three s-like letters: श, ष and स. श and ष sound almost the same today. श is used in most words (शेर, शाम); ष comes mainly in words from Sanskrit.

Short sentences to read

  • शेर शोर करता है।
    The lion makes a noise.
  • शाम को शंख बजता है।
    The conch is blown in the evening.

Questions

How do you write श barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of श are: sha, shaa, shi, shee, shu, shoo, she, shai, sho, shau, shan, shah.

What sound does श make?

The sound of श is like the "sh" in "ship".
