About the letter त्र

त्र belongs to the group संयुक्त अक्षर of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is a soft "t" and "r" together, like "tr" in Spanish "tres". This is not a single sound but two consonants written as one shape, so the mouth moves through both.

Schools teach it with the alphabet because it is so common, but it is a joined letter (संयुक्त अक्षर), built from two consonants. Its twelve forms work like those of any single letter: the matra is added to the whole shape.

Only 6 of the twelve forms of त्र appear in everyday words; the list above shows them, and in some of them the form comes in the middle or at the end of the word rather than at the start. The remaining forms are for chart practice only.

Watch out

त्र is त् and र joined. Do not read it as ट्र (the hard sound in ट्रक). मित्र, पत्र and चित्र all end with the soft त्र.

Short sentences to read

  • मित्र ने पत्र लिखा।
    The friend wrote a letter.
  • त्रिभुज में तीन भुजाएँ होती हैं।
    A triangle has three sides.

Questions

How do you write त्र barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of त्र are: tra, traa, tri, tree, tru, troo, tre, trai, tro, trau, tran, trah.

What sound does त्र make?

The sound of त्र is a soft "t" and "r" together, like "tr" in Spanish "tres".
