About the letter ज्ञ

ज्ञ belongs to the group संयुक्त अक्षर of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is in Hindi it is said "gy", as in "gyaan". This is not a single sound but two consonants written as one shape, so the mouth moves through both.

Schools teach it with the alphabet because it is so common, but it is a joined letter (संयुक्त अक्षर), built from two consonants. Its twelve forms work like those of any single letter: the matra is added to the whole shape.

Only 2 of the twelve forms of ज्ञ appear in everyday words; the list above shows them, and in some of them the form comes in the middle or at the end of the word rather than at the start. The remaining forms are for chart practice only.

Watch out

ज्ञ is written as ज् and ञ joined, but Hindi speakers say "gya". So ज्ञान is read "gyaan", not "jnaan". In Roman letters people write it "gya" or "gyan".

Short sentences to read

  • ज्ञान सबसे बड़ा धन है।
    Knowledge is the greatest wealth.
  • विज्ञान मेरा प्रिय विषय है।
    Science is my favourite subject.

Questions

How do you write ज्ञ barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of ज्ञ are: gya, gyaa, gyi, gyee, gyu, gyoo, gye, gyai, gyo, gyau, gyan, gyah.

What sound does ज्ञ make?

The sound of ज्ञ is in Hindi it is said "gy", as in "gyaan".
