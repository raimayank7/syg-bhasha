About the letter ध

ध belongs to the group त वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is the soft "d" of द with breath. The tip of the tongue touches the back of the upper front teeth. This row is called दंत्य, and its sounds are softer than their English cousins.

It is the fourth letter of its row: voiced like द, with breath added. English has no such sound, so learners often say द instead. Say द, then say it again while breathing out, without putting a vowel between the two parts.

8 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

ध and घ: ध has a curl at the top left and a broken top line. घ has a full top line. Check the top line before you read the word.

Short sentences to read

  • धोबी धीरे चलता है।
    The washerman walks slowly.
  • धूप तेज़ है।
    The sunshine is strong.

Questions

How do you write ध barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of ध are: dha, dhaa, dhi, dhee, dhu, dhoo, dhe, dhai, dho, dhau, dhan, dhah.

What sound does ध make?

The sound of ध is the soft "d" of द with breath.
