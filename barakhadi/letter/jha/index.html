About the letter झ

झ belongs to the group च वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is a "j" with breath, like "dge" + "h" in "hedgehog". The flat middle of the tongue presses against the hard palate, just behind the ridge of the upper teeth. This row is called तालव्य.

It is the fourth letter of its row: voiced like ज, with breath added. English has no such sound, so learners often say ज instead. Say ज, then say it again while breathing out, without putting a vowel between the two parts.

  • jheeबड़ी ई की मात्राझीलlake

  • jhooबड़े ऊ की मात्राझूलाswing
  • jheए की मात्राझेलनाto bear
  • jhaiऐ की मात्राno everyday word
  • jhoओ की मात्राझोलाcloth bag
  • jhauऔ की मात्राno everyday word
  • jhanअनुस्वार (बिंदी)झंडाflag
  • jhahविसर्गno everyday word

9 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

झ has two accepted shapes in print. The common school shape looks like इ with a stick; an older shape looks like भ turned around. Both are the same letter.

Short sentences to read

  • झूला झूलो।
    Swing on the swing.
  • झील के पास झरना है।
    There is a waterfall near the lake.

Questions

How do you write झ barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of झ are: jha, jhaa, jhi, jhee, jhu, jhoo, jhe, jhai, jho, jhau, jhan, jhah.

What sound does झ make?

The sound of झ is a "j" with breath, like "dge" + "h" in "hedgehog".
