क की बारहखड़ी (Ka barakhadi)

The twelve forms of the Hindi letter क, from क to कः, with the way each is said, an example word for the forms that have one, short sentences to read and a line to trace.

About the letter क

क belongs to the group क वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is like the "k" in "skate": no puff of air after it. The back of the tongue rises and touches the soft back part of the roof of the mouth, close to the throat. Grammar books call this row कंठ्य.

It is the first letter of its row, the plain one: the throat does not hum and no puff of breath follows. Hold your palm in front of your mouth and say क: you should feel almost no air. Its breathy partner is ख, and its voiced partner is ग.

The twelve forms with words

Tap a form to hear it. Under each form you see how it is written in English letters, the name of its matra and an example word.

  • kaअ (कोई मात्रा नहीं)कमलlotus
  • kaaआ की मात्राकामwork
  • kiछोटी इ की मात्राकिताबbook
  • keeबड़ी ई की मात्राकीमतprice
  • kuछोटे उ की मात्राकुर्सीchair
  • kooबड़े ऊ की मात्राकूड़ाrubbish
  • keए की मात्राकेलाbanana
  • kaiऐ की मात्राकैंचीscissors
  • koओ की मात्राकोयलcuckoo
  • kauऔ की मात्राकौआcrow
  • kanअनुस्वार (बिंदी)कंघीcomb
  • kahविसर्गno everyday word

11 of the twelve forms have an everyday word in the list above. Read the form first, then the word, then cover the meaning and see if the child remembers it.

Watch out

क and फ look alike. क has its loop on the right closed against the stick; फ has an extra curl that opens to the right. Also keep क (no breath) apart from ख (with breath).

Short sentences to read

  • कमला ने काका को केला दिया।
    Kamla gave a banana to Uncle.
  • कौआ काला होता है।
    A crow is black.

Questions

How do you write क barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of क are: ka, kaa, ki, kee, ku, koo, ke, kai, ko, kau, kan, kah.

What sound does क make?

The sound of क is like the "k" in "skate": no puff of air after it.
