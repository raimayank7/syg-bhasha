About the letter ञ

ञ belongs to the group च वर्ग of the Hindi alphabet. Its sound is the "ny" in "canyon". The flat middle of the tongue presses against the hard palate, just behind the ridge of the upper teeth. This row is called तालव्य.

It is the fifth letter of its row, the nasal: the mouth takes the same position as for च, but the air goes out through the nose. Pinch your nose while saying it and the sound stops.

No everyday Hindi word begins with ञ, and modern Hindi spelling rarely writes this letter with a matra at all. Its row is in the chart so that the table is complete and so that children can practise joining signs to the shape. You do not need to find words for it. Learn to recognise the letter, say its sound, and move on to the next row.

Watch out

No common Hindi word begins with ञ. Its sound appears before च and ज, as in चंचल and पंजा, where modern spelling writes a bindi instead.

Questions

How do you write ञ barakhadi in English?

In simple English letters the twelve forms of ञ are: nya, nyaa, nyi, nyee, nyu, nyoo, nye, nyai, nyo, nyau, nyan, nyah.

What sound does ञ make?

The sound of ञ is the "ny" in "canyon".
