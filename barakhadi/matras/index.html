Barakhadi

The twelve matras

One page for every sign of the barakhadi: where it sits on the letter, how it sounds, words and sentences.

  • क अ (कोई मात्रा नहीं) a · sounds like the short "a" in "about"
  • का आ की मात्रा aa · sounds like the long "a" in "father"
  • कि छोटी इ की मात्रा i · sounds like the short "i" in "pin"
  • की बड़ी ई की मात्रा ee · sounds like the long "ee" in "feet"
  • कु छोटे उ की मात्रा u · sounds like the short "u" in "put"
  • कू बड़े ऊ की मात्रा oo · sounds like the long "oo" in "moon"
  • के ए की मात्रा e · sounds like the "ay" in "day", said without gliding
  • कै ऐ की मात्रा ai · sounds like the "a" in "cat", a little longer
  • को ओ की मात्रा o · sounds like the "o" in "go", said without gliding
  • कौ औ की मात्रा au · sounds like the "aw" in "saw"
  • कं अनुस्वार (बिंदी) an · sounds like a nasal sound like the "n" in "sing" or "m" in "lamp"
  • कः विसर्ग ah · sounds like a soft breath, like a light "h" after the vowel

What a matra is

Hindi has two shapes for every vowel. At the start of a word, or after another vowel, the vowel is written as a full letter: आम, ईख, ऊन. After a consonant it shrinks to a small sign, the matra, which is fixed to that consonant: काम, खीर, दून. The consonant and its matra are read together as one beat. That beat, the syllable, is the real unit of Hindi reading.

Four places a sign can sit

  • After the letter: ा ी ो ौ ः
  • Before the letter: ि only
  • Below the letter: ु ू
  • Above the letter: े ै ं

The signs for ओ and औ are made by combining two of these: the stick after the letter plus the strokes above. Seeing this saves a child from learning them as two more shapes.

Short and long

Six of the vowels form three short and long pairs: अ and आ, इ and ई, उ and ऊ. The long one is held for about twice as long. Hindi spelling depends on this difference, and many pairs of words differ only by length, such as कम (less) and काम (work), दिन (day) and दीन (poor). The vowels ए, ऐ, ओ and औ are always long.

An order for teaching

The chart order works well: आ first, because its sign is the simplest; then the pair इ and ई; then उ and ऊ; then ए and ऐ; then ओ and औ; and last the dot and the two dots. Spend about a week on each. The guide for parents, how to teach barakhadi at home, gives the daily routine, and the page on other signs covers ऋ, chandrabindu and the nukta, which are not part of the twelve.
