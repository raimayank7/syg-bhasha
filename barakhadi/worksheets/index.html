Barakhadi

Barakhadi practice sheets

Choose a letter and a kind of sheet, then print it on A4 paper. Each sheet is made in your browser.

The three kinds of sheet

Trace and write. The row is printed once in solid black as a model, once in grey outline for the child to go over with a pencil, and then an empty line follows for writing it freehand. The twelve forms are split into two halves so that the letters are large enough for small hands.

Fill the blanks. The twelve boxes of the row are printed with five of them left empty. The child writes the missing forms. Because the empty boxes are chosen afresh each time, "Make another" gives a new sheet for the same letter, and a child cannot simply remember where the gaps were.

Match word and meaning. Up to eight words that use the letter are listed on the left and their English meanings, in mixed order, on the right. The child draws lines between them. This sheet suits children who already read the forms and are building vocabulary. A few letters, such as ङ and ञ, have too few words for it.

Printing tips

  • Use A4 paper in portrait. Only the white sheet is printed; the menus and buttons are left out.
  • In the print window, turn off "Headers and footers" for a cleaner page.
  • To save paper, laminate one tracing sheet or slip it into a clear folder and use a whiteboard marker. It wipes clean.
  • The letters use the Hindi font of your device, so the shape may differ slightly between a phone and a computer. Both are correct.

How much practice is enough?

One sheet a day is plenty for a child of five or six. Tracing teaches the hand; it does not teach reading. After the sheet is done, ask the child to read the row aloud and to point to three forms that you name out of order. When a row is written neatly without the grey guide, move to the next letter rather than repeating it. For the order of letters and a full plan, see how to teach barakhadi at home.

For teachers

The sheets carry a line for the name and date, so they can be used as classwork or homework. You may print as many copies as you need for your class. For a wall chart, print the full chart from the home page in landscape.
