अ (कोई मात्रा नहीं): A (no matra)

How the sign of अ is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 34 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

क ख ग घ च छ ज झsounds like the short "a" in "about"

Where the sign goes

The first form has no sign at all. Every Hindi consonant already carries the short vowel अ inside it, so क is read "ka", not "k". That is why the first box of every barakhadi row looks like the bare letter.

How to write it

Write the letter and stop. There is nothing to add. If you want the consonant without any vowel, you add a small slanting stroke called halant below it (क्), which is how half sounds are shown.

The sound is the short "a" in "about".

The usual mistake

The hidden अ is not spoken at the end of most Hindi words. कमल is read "kamal", not "kamala", and घर is "ghar". Children who have just learnt the chart often add the extra "a" at the end; reading whole words aloud cures it.

Sentences to read

  • कमल जल पर है।
    The lotus is on the water.
  • नल बंद कर।
    Turn off the tap.
  • घर चल।
    Come home.
