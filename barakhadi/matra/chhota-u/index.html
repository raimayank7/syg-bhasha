छोटे उ की मात्रा: Chhota u matra

How the sign of उ is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 29 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

कु खु गु घु चु छु जु झुsounds like the short "u" in "put"

Where the sign goes

The sign for short उ (ु) hangs below the letter, like a small hook that opens to the left.

How to write it

Write the consonant, then from the bottom of its stick draw a small curl that turns left. For letters without a stick at the bottom centre, such as ट or द, the curl hangs from the lowest point of the letter.

The sound is the short "u" in "put".

The usual mistake

र is the exception: its short उ is written on the side, as रु, not below. Also keep ु and ू apart: बुरा (bad) and बूरा (powdered sugar) differ only by this sign.

Sentences to read

  • गुलाब सुंदर है।
    The rose is beautiful.
  • कुछ सुन।
    Listen to something.
  • तुम चुप रहो।
    You stay quiet.
