विसर्ग: Visarga (ah)

How the sign of अः is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 5 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

कः खः गः घः चः छः जः झःsounds like a soft breath, like a light "h" after the vowel

Where the sign goes

The twelfth form adds two dots after the letter, one above the other (ः). This sign is called visarga and adds a soft breath after the vowel.

How to write it

Write the letter, then put two small dots to its right, like a colon. Do not use the keyboard colon in typed Hindi; the visarga is its own character.

The sound is a soft breath, like a light "h" after the vowel.

Words with this sign

These 5 words come from the letter pages of this site. In most of them the form stands at the start of the word.

  • छः six
  • अतः therefore
  • पुनः again
  • नमः salutation
  • प्रायः often

The usual mistake

Visarga comes mostly in words taken from Sanskrit: अतः, प्रातः, दुःख, छः. That is why most boxes of this column have no everyday word. Children should know the shape and the sound, and need not hunt for words.

Sentences to read

  • प्रातः उठो।
    Get up early in the morning.
  • छः बज गए।
    It is six o'clock.
  • दुःख मत करो।
    Do not be sad.
