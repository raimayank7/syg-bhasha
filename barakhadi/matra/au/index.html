औ की मात्रा: Au matra

How the sign of औ is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 21 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

कौ खौ गौ घौ चौ छौ जौ झौsounds like the "aw" in "saw"

Where the sign goes

The sign for औ (ौ) is a stick after the letter with two slanting strokes on top of that stick.

How to write it

Write the consonant, add the stick on the right, then put two short strokes above the stick. It is the आ stick plus the ऐ strokes.

The sound is the "aw" in "saw".

The usual mistake

The sound is one open vowel, close to "aw" in English "saw": कौन, चौक, दौड़. In Roman letters it is written "au", which makes some learners say "a-oo". Listen to कौआ and copy the sound.

Sentences to read

  • कौआ कौन ले गया?
    Who took the crow away?
  • चौक पर दौड़ है।
    There is a race at the square.
  • मौसम अच्छा है।
    The weather is good.
