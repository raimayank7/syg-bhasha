ऐ की मात्रा: Ai matra

How the sign of ऐ is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 24 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

कै खै गै घै चै छै जै झैsounds like the "a" in "cat", a little longer

Where the sign goes

The sign for ऐ (ै) is two slanting strokes on top of the letter, side by side, both leaning to the left.

How to write it

Write the consonant, then add two short parallel strokes above the top line. Keep them close together so they read as one sign.

The sound is the "a" in "cat", a little longer.

The usual mistake

The sound of ऐ in standard Hindi is a single open vowel, close to the "a" in English "cat" held a little longer, not "aa-ee". पैसा, बैल and है all have it. Do not drop one stroke, or the word changes.

Sentences to read

  • थैले में पैसे हैं।
    There is money in the bag.
  • बैल खेत में है।
    The ox is in the field.
  • मैदान में खेल है।
    There is a game in the ground.
