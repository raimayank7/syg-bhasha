बड़े ऊ की मात्रा: Bada oo matra

How the sign of ऊ is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 26 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

कू खू गू घू चू छू जू झूsounds like the long "oo" in "moon"

Where the sign goes

The sign for long ऊ (ू) also hangs below the letter, but its curl turns the other way and opens to the right, like a small tail.

How to write it

Write the consonant, then draw a curl below it that swings to the right. A simple memory aid for children: the short one curls back, the long one runs ahead.

The sound is the long "oo" in "moon".

The usual mistake

With र the sign again goes on the side: रू, with a longer curl than रु. Compare रुक (stop) with रूप (form). In small print the two look close, so check the word, not only the shape.

Sentences to read

  • फूल खूब खिले।
    The flowers bloomed a lot.
  • दूध पी लो।
    Drink the milk.
  • सूरज दूर है।
    The sun is far away.
