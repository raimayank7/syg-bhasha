ओ की मात्रा: O matra

How the sign of ओ is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 29 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

को खो गो घो चो छो जो झोsounds like the "o" in "go", said without gliding

Where the sign goes

The sign for ओ (ो) is a stick after the letter with one slanting stroke on top of that stick.

How to write it

Think of it as the आ stick plus the ए stroke. Write the consonant, add the stick on the right, then put one slanting stroke above the stick.

The sound is the "o" in "go", said without gliding.

The usual mistake

One stroke on the stick is ओ, two strokes are औ. मोर (peacock) and मौर (a crown of flowers) differ only there. Also do not confuse ो with ॉ, the half-moon sign used for English words like डॉक्टर.

Sentences to read

  • मोर को देखो।
    Look at the peacock.
  • तोता बोला।
    The parrot spoke.
  • सोनू रोटी लो।
    Sonu, take a roti.
