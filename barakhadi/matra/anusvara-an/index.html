अनुस्वार (बिंदी): Anusvara (an)

How the sign of अं is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 28 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

कं खं गं घं चं छं जं झंsounds like a nasal sound like the "n" in "sing" or "m" in "lamp"

Where the sign goes

The eleventh form adds a dot above the letter. The dot is called anusvara, or simply bindi (ं). It gives the vowel a nasal ending.

How to write it

Write the letter with any matra it needs, then put a clear round dot above the top line, towards the right. The dot is small but it must be visible: गगा and गंगा are not the same.

The sound is a nasal sound like the "n" in "sing" or "m" in "lamp".

The usual mistake

The dot takes the sound of the nasal that matches the next letter: "ng" in गंगा, "n" in बंदर, "m" in खंभा. The half-moon with a dot (ँ, chandrabindu) is a different sign: it makes the vowel itself nasal, as in माँ and चाँद.

Sentences to read

  • बंदर पतंग ले गया।
    The monkey took the kite away.
  • मंदिर में घंटी बजी।
    The bell rang in the temple.
  • रंग लाल है।
    The colour is red.
