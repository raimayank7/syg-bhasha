आ की मात्रा: Aa matra

How the sign of आ is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 34 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

का खा गा घा चा छा जा झाsounds like the long "a" in "father"

Where the sign goes

The sign for आ is one straight stick (ा) placed after the letter, on its right, with the top line running across both.

How to write it

Write the consonant first, then draw a stick of the same height just after it, and take the top line over both. In handwriting the stick is drawn from top to bottom.

The sound is the long "a" in "father".

The usual mistake

The stick of आ looks exactly like the stick that many letters already have. Count the sticks: प has one stick, पा has two. For round letters like ट and द the added stick stands a little apart: टा, दा.

Sentences to read

  • काका आया।
    Uncle came.
  • नाना का बाग बड़ा है।
    Nana's garden is big.
  • माला लाल है।
    The garland is red.
