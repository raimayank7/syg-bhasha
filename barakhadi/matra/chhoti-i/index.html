छोटी इ की मात्रा: Chhoti i matra

How the sign of इ is written on every Hindi letter, how it sounds, 31 everyday words that use it and short sentences to read.

कि खि गि घि चि छि जि झिsounds like the short "i" in "pin"

Where the sign goes

The sign for short इ (ि) is the odd one out: it is written before the letter, on the left, although the sound is spoken after the consonant. Its curved flag goes over the top line and comes down on the left.

How to write it

In handwriting many teachers ask children to write the consonant first and then add the stick on the left with the flag reaching over to touch the letter. On a keyboard you also type the consonant first and the matra second.

The sound is the short "i" in "pin".

The usual mistake

The classic mistake is mixing ि and ी. Remember: छोटी इ comes before the letter and is said quickly; बड़ी ई comes after the letter and is held longer. दिन (day) and दीन (poor) are different words.

Sentences to read

  • किताब मिली।
    The book was found.
  • दिन निकला।
    The day has begun.
  • चिड़िया गिरी नहीं।
    The bird did not fall.
