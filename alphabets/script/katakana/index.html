Katakana: letters, names and quiz

46 symbols of the Katakana script (カタカナ), used for Japanese (foreign words and names).

Katakana is the other Japanese syllable script, used for foreign words and names, sounds and emphasis. Its 46 basic symbols have the same sounds as the hiragana but angular shapes.

The chart lists them in the traditional gojuon order. A long vowel is written with a dash, ー. Learn katakana after hiragana, and you can read menus and foreign names in Japan.

How this chart was made: the letters and their names are read from the Unicode Character Database (version 16.0.0), so they are not typed by hand. For this script the label is the syllable as given in its Unicode name. The sound labels are approximate and no native reader has checked this page.
