Kannada: letters, names and quiz

55 symbols of the Kannada script (ಕನ್ನಡ), used for Kannada.

Kannada is the script of Karnataka. Its letters are round and loopy, and it is a sister of the Telugu script. It is written from left to right. It is an abugida: Each consonant letter carries a built-in vowel (usually "a"). Other vowels are shown by signs written around the consonant, and a special sign (the virama or its equivalent) removes the vowel so that two consonants can join.

The chart below shows the vowel letters, the consonants and the vowel signs in the Unicode order, which is also the traditional order.

How this chart was made: the letters and their names are read from the Unicode Character Database (version 16.0.0), so they are not typed by hand. For this script the label is the usual Latin spelling of the letter's sound as it appears in its Unicode name. The sound labels are approximate and no native reader has checked this page.

How to learn it

  1. Learn the vowel letters and signs.
  2. Practise the consonants in rows.
  3. Compare with Telugu to see the family likeness.

Questions

Can I read Telugu if I know Kannada?

The letters are close, but the languages differ, so you would read the sounds without understanding the words.
