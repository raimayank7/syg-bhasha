Malayalam: letters, names and quiz

55 symbols of the Malayalam script (മലയാളം), used for Malayalam.

Malayalam is the script of Kerala. Its letters are circular and it keeps many letters for the sounds of Sanskrit as well as its own. It is written from left to right. It is an abugida: Each consonant letter carries a built-in vowel (usually "a"). Other vowels are shown by signs written around the consonant, and a special sign (the virama or its equivalent) removes the vowel so that two consonants can join.

Old Malayalam printing used hundreds of joined letters; modern printing uses a simplified set. The chart below shows the basic vowels, consonants and signs.

How this chart was made: the letters and their names are read from the Unicode Character Database (version 16.0.0), so they are not typed by hand. For this script the label is the usual Latin spelling of the letter's sound as it appears in its Unicode name. The sound labels are approximate and no native reader has checked this page.

How to learn it

  1. Learn the vowel letters and signs.
  2. Learn the consonant rows.
  3. Practise circles: many letters differ in the direction of one loop.

Questions

Is Malayalam hard to write?

The loops take practice, but the sounds are regular, so reading comes quickly.
