Hebrew: letters, names and quiz

27 symbols of the Hebrew script (עברית), used for Hebrew.

Hebrew is written from right to left. It is an abjad: it writes consonants, and short vowels are shown by dots and dashes that are usually left out. The chart shows the 22 letters and the five final forms used at the end of a word.

Hebrew letters do not join. Five letters (כ מ נ פ צ) have a different shape at the end of a word, and the chart lists them as separate letters, so it has 27 entries. The Latin equivalent comes from the unidecode tables and is approximate.

How this chart was made: the letters and their names are read from the Unicode Character Database (version 16.0.0), so they are not typed by hand. The Latin equivalent comes from the transliterate or unidecode tables and is a guide only. The sound labels are approximate and no native reader has checked this page.

How to learn it

  1. Learn the letters in order, five at a time.
  2. Learn the five final forms.
  3. Read short signs: שלום (shalom).
