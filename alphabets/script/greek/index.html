Greek: letters, names and quiz

24 symbols of the Greek script (Ελληνικά), used for Greek.

The Greek alphabet has 24 letters. It is written from left to right and gave rise to the Latin and Cyrillic alphabets. It is often described as the first alphabet to give vowels their own letters.

Each letter has an upper-case and a lower-case form, shown together in the chart. The Latin equivalent is the modern Greek sound as given by the transliterate package; the ancient sounds differed. At the end of a word, the lower-case sigma is written ς instead of σ.

How this chart was made: the letters and their names are read from the Unicode Character Database (version 16.0.0), so they are not typed by hand. The Latin equivalent comes from the transliterate or unidecode tables and is a guide only. The sound labels are approximate and no native reader has checked this page.

How to learn it

  1. Learn the alphabet in four groups of six.
  2. You already know many of the shapes from mathematics: α β γ δ π σ ω.
  3. Read street signs: many Greek words are close to English.

Questions

How long does it take to learn the Greek alphabet?

Most people learn it in one or two evenings.
