Telugu: letters, names and quiz

55 symbols of the Telugu script (తెలుగు), used for Telugu.

Telugu is the script of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Its letters are round, and it is closely related to Kannada because both grew from an old shared script. It is written from left to right. It is an abugida: Each consonant letter carries a built-in vowel (usually "a"). Other vowels are shown by signs written around the consonant, and a special sign (the virama or its equivalent) removes the vowel so that two consonants can join.

The chart below shows the vowel letters, the consonants and the signs. Telugu words usually end in a vowel, which makes the language sound smooth.

How this chart was made: the letters and their names are read from the Unicode Character Database (version 16.0.0), so they are not typed by hand. For this script the label is the usual Latin spelling of the letter's sound as it appears in its Unicode name. The sound labels are approximate and no native reader has checked this page.

How to learn it

  1. Learn the vowel letters and signs together.
  2. If you know Kannada, notice how many Telugu letters look alike.
  3. Practise the rounded shapes by tracing.

Questions

Is Telugu like Kannada?

The scripts look very alike. The languages are different but related.
