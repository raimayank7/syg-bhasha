Tamil: letters, names and quiz

41 symbols of the Tamil script (தமிழ்), used for Tamil.

Tamil is the script of one of the oldest living classical languages. It is written from left to right and is an abugida, but a smaller one than the northern scripts: Each consonant letter carries a built-in vowel (usually "a"). Other vowels are shown by signs written around the consonant, and a special sign (the virama or its equivalent) removes the vowel so that two consonants can join. Tamil has 12 vowels and 18 core consonants.

Tamil has no separate letters for voiced and aspirated sounds, so one letter, க, is read as k or g depending on its place in a word. It also has special letters, such as ழ and ற, with sounds that other Indian languages do not have. Grantha letters are added to write Sanskrit words.

How this chart was made: the letters and their names are read from the Unicode Character Database (version 16.0.0), so they are not typed by hand. For this script the label is the usual Latin spelling of the letter's sound as it appears in its Unicode name. The sound labels are approximate and no native reader has checked this page.

How to learn it

  1. Learn the 12 vowels and the 18 consonants first: that is the whole core.
  2. Learn the vowel signs with க.
  3. Learn how the sound of a letter changes with its place in a word, by listening to a speaker.

Questions

How many letters does Tamil have?

The core is 12 vowels and 18 consonants. The combined letters (consonant plus vowel) number 216 in the traditional count.
