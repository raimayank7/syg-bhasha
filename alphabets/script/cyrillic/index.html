Cyrillic (Russian): letters, names and quiz

33 symbols of the Cyrillic script (Кириллица), used for Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Serbian and others.

Cyrillic is the alphabet of Russian and many other languages. The chart shows the 33 letters of the Russian alphabet. It is written from left to right and is based on the Greek alphabet.

Some letters look like Latin letters but sound different: В is v, Н is n, Р is r, С is s. Some letters have no Latin look at all (Ж, Ш, Щ, Ы). The Latin equivalent is taken from the transliterate package. Two letters, Ъ and Ь, have no sound of their own and change the letters beside them.

How this chart was made: the letters and their names are read from the Unicode Character Database (version 16.0.0), so they are not typed by hand. The Latin equivalent comes from the transliterate or unidecode tables and is a guide only. The sound labels are approximate and no native reader has checked this page.

How to learn it

  1. Start with the letters that look like Latin ones and sound alike: А К М О Т.
  2. Then learn the false friends: В Н Р С У Х.
  3. Read city names on maps: Москва, Санкт-Петербург.

Questions

Is Russian Cyrillic the same as Bulgarian?

The alphabets are close but not identical. Each language has its own set of letters.
