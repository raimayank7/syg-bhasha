Georgian: letters, names and quiz

33 symbols of the Georgian script (ქართული), used for Georgian.

The Georgian alphabet (Mkhedruli) has 33 letters. It is written from left to right and, in everyday writing, has no capital letters. Each letter stands for one sound, and it is read as it is written.

The letters are round and flowing. The chart shows the letters in order with their Unicode names (which are the traditional letter names) and the Latin equivalent from the transliterate package.

How this chart was made: the letters and their names are read from the Unicode Character Database (version 16.0.0), so they are not typed by hand. The Latin equivalent comes from the transliterate or unidecode tables and is a guide only. The sound labels are approximate and no native reader has checked this page.

How to learn it

  1. Learn the alphabet in order, five at a time.
  2. Notice that everyday writing has no upper case.
  3. Read place names: თბილისი (Tbilisi).
