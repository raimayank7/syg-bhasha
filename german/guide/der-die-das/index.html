Der, die, das: how to learn the gender of German nouns

Every German noun is masculine (der), feminine (die) or neuter (das). The gender is not about meaning in most cases: das Mädchen (the girl) is neuter, and der Löffel (the spoon) is masculine. Learners must learn the article with the noun.

The golden rule: learn the article with the noun

Do not write "Haus" in your vocabulary list. Write "das Haus, die Häuser". The article and the plural are part of the word. Colour-code if you like: blue for der, red for die, green for das.

Patterns that help

  • Masculine: male people and animals (der Mann, der Vater); days, months and seasons (der Montag, der Juli, der Winter); words that end in -er for people who do something (der Lehrer); many words ending in -ismus, -ling, -or.
  • Feminine: female people (die Frau, die Mutter); most words ending in -e (die Schule, die Blume, die Straße); words ending in -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -ion, -tät, -ik (die Zeitung, die Freiheit, die Universität, die Musik); many numbers as nouns (die Eins).
  • Neuter: words ending in -chen and -lein (das Mädchen, das Fräulein); infinitives used as nouns (das Essen, das Leben); most words ending in -ment, -um (das Dokument, das Datum); many metals and chemical elements (das Gold).

Exceptions

All of these rules have exceptions. Some words with -e are masculine (der Name, der Käse), and some words for people are not the gender you expect. Treat the patterns as a guide for new words, and check a dictionary.

Plurals

In the plural all genders use die: der Mann becomes die Männer, das Kind becomes die Kinder. Plurals follow several patterns (-e, -er, -n, -s, umlaut, no change) and are best learned with the noun. The noun list on this site shows the plural of each word.

Practise

Use the noun list and the article quiz. Say the whole phrase aloud ("der Tisch, die Tische"), not just the word.

Questions

Is there a rule for which nouns are masculine?

There are patterns (days, months, many -er words for people), but no rule that works for all nouns, so learn each noun with its article.

Why is "das Mädchen" neuter?

Because words ending in -chen are always neuter in German, whatever they mean.
