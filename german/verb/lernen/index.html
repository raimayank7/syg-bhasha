lernen: conjugation

to learn

lernen means to learn. It is a weak (regular) verb. The past participle is gelernt, and the perfect tense is made with haben.

Weak verbs add -e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en to the stem in the present and -te, -test, -te, -ten, -tet, -ten in the preterite, and the participle is ge- + stem + -t.

Future: werden + the infinitive. See haben or sein for the perfect and word order for where the verb goes.
