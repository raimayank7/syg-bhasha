Words for in-laws in Hindi, Punjabi, Bengali and Urdu

Marriage adds a new set of relatives, and the languages of South Asia have a word for each of them. These words carry feelings of respect, joking and affection, so it helps to know the tone.

The spouse's parents

The husband's or wife's father is called sasur in Hindi (ससुर) and shoshur in Bengali, and the mother saas (सास) or shashuri. These are respectful words. Many people also use the words for "father" and "mother" with the in-laws, or a title such as papa-ji or mummy-ji.

Brothers and sisters of the spouse

  • A wife's brother is a sala and a wife's sister is a sali. The words are used in affection and joking between a man and his wife's family, but sala can also be used as an insult in some places, so it is best not to use it for a stranger.
  • A man's brother's wife is a bhabhi (Hindi, Urdu) or boudi (Bengali). It is a respectful and affectionate word, and many people use bhabhi for a friend's wife or an older woman.
  • A sister's husband is a jija (Hindi), jeeja (Punjabi) or jamaibabu (Bengali), who is treated with respect and also teased.

The children's spouses

A son's wife is a bahu in Hindi and Urdu (بہو), nuh in Punjabi (ਨੂੰਹ) and bouma in Bengali. A daughter's husband is a damad (Hindi and Urdu) or jamai (Bengali).

Using the words

  • Use the word with the name and a respectful ending when you speak to an elder, for example "Bhabhi ji" or "Chacha ji".
  • Do not use the in-law words to someone you have just met unless the relationship is clear.
  • When in doubt, ask what the person would like to be called.

Questions

Is "sala" an insult?

Between a man and his wife's brother it is an affectionate or joking word, but in other settings it can be an insult, so do not use it casually.

What do I call my husband's elder brother?

There are separate words for it in each language (for example jeth in Hindi). This chart covers the main relationships only and does not list every in-law term.
