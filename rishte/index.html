How to read the chart

Each term has a badge. checked means the term was found in the English Wiktionary with a meaning that names the relationship (89 of 112). single source means we could not confirm it, so ask a speaker of the language. Read how kinship terms work for the pattern behind the words, and in-law terms for the words you gain through marriage.

Questions

Why four languages?

They are the four we could check with the sources available. More languages would need a native reader for each.

Are there words for older and younger relatives?

Yes, in the real languages. Where they exist, this chart gives one common word, and the guide explains the distinction.
