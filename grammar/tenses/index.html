The twelve English tenses

Each tense has its own page with form, uses, time words and examples.

  • Simple presentbase verb (add -s or -es for he, she, it)
  • Present continuousam, is or are + verb-ing
  • Present perfecthave or has + past participle
  • Present perfect continuoushave or has + been + verb-ing
  • Simple pastpast form of the verb (-ed for regular verbs)
  • Past continuouswas or were + verb-ing
  • Past perfecthad + past participle
  • Past perfect continuoushad been + verb-ing
  • Simple futurewill + base verb
  • Future continuouswill be + verb-ing
  • Future perfectwill have + past participle
  • Future perfect continuouswill have been + verb-ing

How the tenses fit together

English tenses combine a time (present, past or future) with an aspect (simple, continuous, perfect or perfect continuous). The simple forms state facts and habits, the continuous forms show an action in progress, the perfect forms connect two times, and the perfect continuous forms show how long something has been going on. Learn one time at a time: present first, then past, then future.
