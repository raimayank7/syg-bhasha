Trace a letter

Draw over the grey letter with a finger, a stylus or the mouse.

How to use the tracing pad

  1. Choose Hindi or Punjabi, then a letter. The letter appears large and grey, with its picture word beside its name where it has one.
  2. Let the child draw over the grey shape with one finger. Each time the finger lifts, a stroke ends; "Undo" removes the last stroke and "Clear" wipes the pad.
  3. Tap "Next" to move through the alphabet in order, or pick any letter from the list.

Which way do the strokes go?

The pad does not force an order, because handwriting models differ between schools. As a rule, strokes go from top to bottom and from left to right. In Hindi the body of the letter is written first and the top line last. In Punjabi most teachers write the top line first and hang the letter from it. The page on how to write the letters explains both habits and the families of shapes.

Tips for small hands

  • Turn the phone sideways or use a tablet if you have one: a bigger letter is easier to follow.
  • Start with the thick pen. A thick line hides wobbles and keeps a child keen.
  • Three or four letters in one sitting are enough. Say the sound of the letter aloud each time it is traced.
  • Screen tracing teaches the path of the letter. Holding a pencil is a different skill, so follow up on paper with the printable tracing sheets.

Privacy

What is drawn stays on the screen of your device. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and the pad is cleared when you change the letter or leave the page.
