Punjabi letters ਤ ਥ ਦ ਧ ਨ

Row 5 of the Gurmukhi alphabet: the name, sound and words of each letter, with the matching Hindi letter.

The fifth row is made with the tip of the tongue on the upper teeth: the soft t and d sounds, the same as Hindi त थ द ध न.

The letters of this row are called ਤੱਤਾ (tatta), ਥੱਥਾ (thattha (soft)), ਦੱਦਾ (dadda (soft)), ਧੱਦਾ (dhadda (soft)), ਨੰਨਾ (nanna). Children recite the names in this order, in a sing-song rhythm, long before they can write the shapes.

ਤ ਤੱਤਾ (tatta)

A soft "t" with the tongue on the teeth.

Hindi letter with the same job: त.

Words that begin with it

  • ਤੋਤਾ parrot
  • ਤਿਤਲੀ butterfly
  • ਤਾਰਾ star
  • ਤਰਬੂਜ਼ watermelon

Trace ਤ

ਥ ਥੱਥਾ (thattha (soft))

The soft "t" with a puff of breath. It is not the "th" of English "think".

Hindi letter with the same job: थ.

Words that begin with it

  • ਥਾਲੀ plate
  • ਥੈਲਾ bag
  • ਥੰਮ੍ਹ pillar
  • ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ a little

Trace ਥ

ਦ ਦੱਦਾ (dadda (soft))

A soft "d" with the tongue on the teeth.

Hindi letter with the same job: द.

Words that begin with it

  • ਦੁੱਧ milk
  • ਦੰਦ tooth
  • ਦਵਾਈ medicine
  • ਦਾਦੀ father's mother

Trace ਦ

ਧ ਧੱਦਾ (dhadda (soft))

At the start of a word it is said like ਤ with a low tone. ਧੁੱਪ sounds close to "tupp" said low.

Hindi letter with the same job: ध.

Words that begin with it

  • ਧੁੱਪ sunshine
  • ਧਾਗਾ thread
  • ਧਰਤੀ the earth
  • ਧੀ daughter

Trace ਧ

ਨ ਨੰਨਾ (nanna)

The everyday "n" of "nut".

Hindi letter with the same job: न.

Words that begin with it

  • ਨੱਕ nose
  • ਨਲਕਾ hand pump
  • ਨਦੀ river
  • ਨਾਨੀ mother's mother

Trace ਨ

Writing this row

In Gurmukhi the line on top is usually written first, from left to right, and the body of the letter is then hung from it. This is the opposite of the habit taught for Hindi, where the top line comes last. A few letters have an open top where the line is broken or missing; copy that gap exactly, because it is part of the letter. More on how the letters are written.
