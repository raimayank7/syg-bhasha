Punjabi letters ਸ਼ ਖ਼ ਗ਼ ਜ਼ ਫ਼ ਲ਼

Row 8 of the Gurmukhi alphabet: the name, sound and words of each letter, with the matching Hindi letter.

Six more letters are made by putting a dot at the foot of an existing letter. The dot is called ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ, "dot at the foot". These letters were added to the thirty-five original ones to write sounds that came with Persian, Arabic and later English words. In everyday writing people often leave the dot out, and readers know the word anyway.

The letters of this row are called ਸੱਸੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (sasse pair bindi), ਖੱਖੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (khakhe pair bindi), ਗੱਗੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (gagge pair bindi), ਜੱਜੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (jajje pair bindi), ਫੱਫੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (phaphe pair bindi), ਲੱਲੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (lalle pair bindi). Children recite the names in this order, in a sing-song rhythm, long before they can write the shapes.

ਸ਼ ਸੱਸੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (sasse pair bindi)

The "sh" of "ship". This is the most used of the six.

Hindi letter with the same job: श.

Words that begin with it

  • ਸ਼ੇਰ lion
  • ਸ਼ਹਿਦ honey
  • ਸ਼ੀਸ਼ਾ mirror
  • ਸ਼ਹਿਰ city

Trace ਸ਼

ਖ਼ ਖੱਖੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (khakhe pair bindi)

A rough "kh" made at the back of the throat, as in the Scottish word "loch". Many speakers simply say ਖ.

Hindi letter with the same job: ख़.

Words that begin with it

  • ਖ਼ਬਰ news
  • ਖ਼ਤ letter
  • ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ happy

Trace ਖ਼

ਗ਼ ਗੱਗੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (gagge pair bindi)

A soft gargled "g" from the back of the throat. Many speakers simply say ਗ.

Hindi letter with the same job: ग़.

Words that begin with it

  • ਗ਼ਲਤ wrong
  • ਗ਼ੁਬਾਰਾ balloon
  • ਗ਼ਰੀਬ poor

Trace ਗ਼

ਜ਼ ਜੱਜੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (jajje pair bindi)

The "z" of "zoo".

Hindi letter with the same job: ज़.

Words that begin with it

  • ਜ਼ਮੀਨ land
  • ਜ਼ੈਬਰਾ zebra
  • ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ life
  • ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ a cold

Trace ਜ਼

ਫ਼ ਫੱਫੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (phaphe pair bindi)

The "f" of "fan".

Hindi letter with the same job: फ़.

Words that begin with it

  • ਫ਼ੋਨ phone
  • ਫ਼ੌਜੀ soldier
  • ਫ਼ਰਸ਼ floor
  • ਫ਼ੀਸ fee

Trace ਫ਼

ਲ਼ ਲੱਲੇ ਪੈਰ ਬਿੰਦੀ (lalle pair bindi)

An "l" said with the tongue curled back. It is the newest and least used of the six; many books do not print it, and no word begins with it.

Hindi letter with the same job: ळ.

Words

There are no everyday words to list for this letter.

Trace ਲ਼

Writing this row

In Gurmukhi the line on top is usually written first, from left to right, and the body of the letter is then hung from it. This is the opposite of the habit taught for Hindi, where the top line comes last. A few letters have an open top where the line is broken or missing; copy that gap exactly, because it is part of the letter. More on how the letters are written.
