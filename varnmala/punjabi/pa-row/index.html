Punjabi letters ਪ ਫ ਬ ਭ ਮ

Row 6 of the Gurmukhi alphabet: the name, sound and words of each letter, with the matching Hindi letter.

The sixth row is made with the lips, the same as Hindi प फ ब भ म.

The letters of this row are called ਪੱਪਾ (pappa), ਫੱਫਾ (phapha), ਬੱਬਾ (babba), ਭੱਬਾ (bhabba), ਮੰਮਾ (mamma). Children recite the names in this order, in a sing-song rhythm, long before they can write the shapes.

ਪ ਪੱਪਾ (pappa)

A plain "p" with no puff of breath.

Hindi letter with the same job: प.

Words that begin with it

  • ਪਤੰਗ kite
  • ਪਾਣੀ water
  • ਪੱਖਾ fan
  • ਪਿੰਡ village

Trace ਪ

ਫ ਫੱਫਾ (phapha)

A "p" with a puff of breath. With a dot below it becomes ਫ਼, the English "f".

Hindi letter with the same job: फ.

Words that begin with it

  • ਫੁੱਲ flower
  • ਫਲ fruit
  • ਫੁਲਕਾ thin roti
  • ਫੱਟੀ writing board

Trace ਫ

ਬ ਬੱਬਾ (babba)

The "b" of "bat".

Hindi letter with the same job: ब.

Words that begin with it

  • ਬੱਕਰੀ goat
  • ਬਿੱਲੀ cat
  • ਬਾਂਦਰ monkey
  • ਬੱਦਲ cloud

Trace ਬ

ਭ ਭੱਬਾ (bhabba)

At the start of a word it is said like ਪ with a low tone. ਭੈਣ sounds close to "pain" said low.

Hindi letter with the same job: भ.

Words that begin with it

  • ਭਾਲੂ bear
  • ਭੈਣ sister
  • ਭਿੰਡੀ okra
  • ਭੇਡ sheep

Trace ਭ

ਮ ਮੰਮਾ (mamma)

The "m" of "man".

Hindi letter with the same job: म.

Words that begin with it

  • ਮੋਰ peacock
  • ਮੱਛੀ fish
  • ਮਾਂ mother
  • ਮੱਖਣ butter

Trace ਮ

Writing this row

In Gurmukhi the line on top is usually written first, from left to right, and the body of the letter is then hung from it. This is the opposite of the habit taught for Hindi, where the top line comes last. A few letters have an open top where the line is broken or missing; copy that gap exactly, because it is part of the letter. More on how the letters are written.
