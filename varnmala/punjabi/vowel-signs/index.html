Punjabi vowel signs: lagan matra

The thirty-five letters of the Punjabi alphabet are almost all consonants. Vowels are added with small signs called ਲਗਾਂ ਮਾਤਰਾ (lagan matra). There are ten vowel sounds. Each has a name, and Punjabi children learn the names as carefully as the letters.

The ten vowel sounds on the letter ਕ

  • ਕ mukta (ਮੁਕਤਾ): no sign; the short "a" of "about"
  • ਕਾ kanna (ਕੰਨਾ): a stick after the letter; long "aa"
  • ਕਿ sihari (ਸਿਹਾਰੀ): a flag before the letter; short "i"
  • ਕੀ bihari (ਬਿਹਾਰੀ): a flag after the letter; long "ee"
  • ਕੁ aunkar (ਔਂਕੜ): one small stroke below; short "u"
  • ਕੂ dulainkar (ਦੁਲੈਂਕੜ): two small strokes below; long "oo"
  • ਕੇ lavan (ਲਾਂ): one stroke above; "e" as in "day"
  • ਕੈ dulavan (ਦੁਲਾਵਾਂ): two strokes above; "ai" as in "cat"
  • ਕੋ hora (ਹੋੜਾ): a curved stroke above; "o" as in "go"
  • ਕੌ kanaura (ਕਨੌੜਾ): a curved stroke with a hook above; "au" as in "saw"

"Mukta" means "free": the letter is free of any sign. A reader of Hindi will recognise most of the signs. The first four are almost the same as in Hindi. The signs below and above the letter look a little different, and ੋ and ੌ sit on top of the letter with no stick, unlike Hindi ो and ौ.

Vowels at the start of a word: the three carriers

A sign needs a letter to sit on. When a word begins with a vowel, Punjabi uses one of three carrier letters from the first row of the alphabet:

  • ੳ (oora) carries the lip-rounded vowels: ਉ ਊ ਓ. Examples: ਉੱਲੂ (owl), ਊਠ (camel).
  • ਅ (aira) carries the open vowels: ਅ ਆ ਐ ਔ. Examples: ਅੰਬ (mango), ਆਲੂ (potato), ਐਨਕ (spectacles), ਔਰਤ (woman).
  • ੲ (eeri) carries the spread-lip vowels: ਇ ਈ ਏ. Examples: ਇੱਟ (brick), ਈਦ (Eid), ਏਕਤਾ (unity).

The pairing is fixed. You never write a kanna on ੳ or an aunkar on ੲ. This is the rule beginners break most often, and the one that makes Punjabi spelling look wrong at a glance.

Three more signs

Bindi (ਂ) and tippi (ੰ)

Both make a sound nasal. Which one to use depends on the vowel. Tippi goes with mukta, sihari, aunkar and dulainkar: ਅੰਬ, ਪਿੰਡ, ਮੁੰਡਾ. Bindi goes with kanna, bihari, lavan, dulavan, hora and kanaura: ਮਾਂ, ਨੀਂਦ, ਗੇਂਦ. Vowels written on the carrier ੳ take the bindi as well: ਉਂਗਲ.

Addhak (ੱ)

This small crescent is written above the letter before the one it doubles. It tells you to hold the next consonant a little longer: ਪੱਕਾ (firm), ਸੱਚ (truth), ਹੱਥ (hand). It changes meaning: ਪਤਾ is "address" and ਪੱਤਾ is "leaf". The names of most Punjabi letters carry an addhak themselves: ਕੱਕਾ, ਖੱਖਾ, ਗੱਗਾ.

Small letters at the foot

Three letters can be written small under another letter: ਹ, ਰ and ਵ. The foot ਰ makes a cluster, as in ਪ੍ਰੇਮ (love). The foot ਹ usually marks a tone, as in ਪੜ੍ਹਨਾ (to read). The foot ਵ is rare, as in ਸ੍ਵੈ (self). A beginner can leave these until plain words are read easily.

How to teach the signs

Take them in the order above, a few days for each. Recite the ten forms on one letter as a chant: ਕ ਕਾ ਕਿ ਕੀ ਕੁ ਕੂ ਕੇ ਕੈ ਕੋ ਕੌ. Punjabi teachers call this chart ਮੁਹਾਰਨੀ (muharni). Then read words that use only the new sign. Start the signs only after the letters are known out of order.
