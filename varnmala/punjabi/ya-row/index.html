Punjabi letters ਯ ਰ ਲ ਵ ੜ

Row 7 of the Gurmukhi alphabet: the name, sound and words of each letter, with the matching Hindi letter.

The seventh row holds the flowing sounds. Four of them match Hindi य र ल व. The fifth, ੜ, is a full letter of the Punjabi alphabet with its own place in the chart, while Hindi writes the same sound as ड़, with a dot.

The letters of this row are called ਯਈਆ (yaiya), ਰਾਰਾ (rara), ਲੱਲਾ (lalla), ਵਾਵਾ (vava), ੜਾੜਾ (rara (hard)). Children recite the names in this order, in a sing-song rhythm, long before they can write the shapes.

ਯ ਯਈਆ (yaiya)

The "y" of "yes". Few native Punjabi words begin with it.

Hindi letter with the same job: य.

Words that begin with it

  • ਯਾਦ memory
  • ਯਾਰ friend
  • ਯੱਕਾ horse cart

Trace ਯ

ਰ ਰਾਰਾ (rara)

A tapped "r", made with one quick touch of the tongue.

Hindi letter with the same job: र.

Words that begin with it

  • ਰੋਟੀ roti
  • ਰੇਲ train
  • ਰਾਜਾ king
  • ਰੰਗ colour

Trace ਰ

ਲ ਲੱਲਾ (lalla)

The "l" of "light".

Hindi letter with the same job: ल.

Words that begin with it

  • ਲੱਸੀ lassi
  • ਲਾਲ red
  • ਲੂੰਬੜੀ fox
  • ਲੱਕੜ wood

Trace ਲ

ਵ ਵਾਵਾ (vava)

Between English "v" and "w". Many words that begin with ब in Hindi begin with ਵ in Punjabi.

Hindi letter with the same job: व.

Words that begin with it

  • ਵਾਲ hair
  • ਵੀਰ brother
  • ਵਿਹੜਾ courtyard
  • ਵੱਡਾ big

Trace ਵ

ੜ ੜਾੜਾ (rara (hard))

A quick flap of the curled tongue. It never begins a word.

Hindi letter with the same job: ड़.

Words

  • ਘੋੜਾ horse
  • ਸੜਕ road
  • ਪਹਾੜ mountain
  • ਕੱਪੜਾ cloth

Trace ੜ

Writing this row

In Gurmukhi the line on top is usually written first, from left to right, and the body of the letter is then hung from it. This is the opposite of the habit taught for Hindi, where the top line comes last. A few letters have an open top where the line is broken or missing; copy that gap exactly, because it is part of the letter. More on how the letters are written.
