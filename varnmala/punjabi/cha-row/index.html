Punjabi letters ਚ ਛ ਜ ਝ ਞ

Row 3 of the Gurmukhi alphabet: the name, sound and words of each letter, with the matching Hindi letter.

The third row is made with the middle of the tongue against the palate, like Hindi च छ ज झ ञ. Again the fourth letter, ਝ, is a tone letter in Punjabi.

The letters of this row are called ਚੱਚਾ (chacha), ਛੱਛਾ (chhachha), ਜੱਜਾ (jajja), ਝੱਜਾ (jhajja), ਞੰਞਾ (nyanya). Children recite the names in this order, in a sing-song rhythm, long before they can write the shapes.

ਚ ਚੱਚਾ (chacha)

A light "ch" with no puff of breath.

Hindi letter with the same job: च.

Words that begin with it

  • ਚਿੜੀ sparrow
  • ਚਾਹ tea
  • ਚੰਨ moon
  • ਚਮਚਾ spoon

Trace ਚ

ਛ ਛੱਛਾ (chhachha)

A "ch" with a strong puff of breath.

Hindi letter with the same job: छ.

Words that begin with it

  • ਛਤਰੀ umbrella
  • ਛੱਤ roof
  • ਛੁੱਟੀ holiday
  • ਛੇ six

Trace ਛ

ਜ ਜੱਜਾ (jajja)

The "j" of "jug". With a dot below it becomes ਜ਼, the "z" sound.

Hindi letter with the same job: ज.

Words that begin with it

  • ਜੁੱਤੀ shoe
  • ਜੰਗਲ forest
  • ਜੀਭ tongue
  • ਜਹਾਜ਼ ship, aeroplane

Trace ਜ

ਝ ਝੱਜਾ (jhajja)

At the start of a word it is said like ਚ with a low tone. ਝੰਡਾ sounds close to "chanda" said low.

Hindi letter with the same job: झ.

Words that begin with it

  • ਝੰਡਾ flag
  • ਝਾੜੂ broom
  • ਝੂਲਾ swing
  • ਝੀਲ lake

Trace ਝ

ਞ ਞੰਞਾ (nyanya)

The "ny" of "canyon". No everyday word begins with it and it is rare in writing.

Hindi letter with the same job: ञ.

Words

There are no everyday words to list for this letter.

Trace ਞ

Writing this row

In Gurmukhi the line on top is usually written first, from left to right, and the body of the letter is then hung from it. This is the opposite of the habit taught for Hindi, where the top line comes last. A few letters have an open top where the line is broken or missing; copy that gap exactly, because it is part of the letter. More on how the letters are written.
