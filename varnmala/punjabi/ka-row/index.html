Punjabi letters ਕ ਖ ਗ ਘ ਙ

Row 2 of the Gurmukhi alphabet: the name, sound and words of each letter, with the matching Hindi letter.

The second row is made at the back of the mouth, like the Hindi row क ख ग घ ङ. The first three letters sound the same as in Hindi. The fourth, ਘ, is where Punjabi goes its own way: it is not a breathy "gh" but a tone letter.

The letters of this row are called ਕੱਕਾ (kakka), ਖੱਖਾ (khakha), ਗੱਗਾ (gagga), ਘੱਗਾ (ghagga), ਙੰਙਾ (nganga). Children recite the names in this order, in a sing-song rhythm, long before they can write the shapes.

ਕ ਕੱਕਾ (kakka)

A plain "k" with no puff of breath, as in "skate".

Hindi letter with the same job: क.

Words that begin with it

  • ਕਬੂਤਰ pigeon
  • ਕੇਲਾ banana
  • ਕੰਨ ear
  • ਕਿਤਾਬ book

Trace ਕ

ਖ ਖੱਖਾ (khakha)

A "k" with a clear puff of breath.

Hindi letter with the same job: ख.

Words that begin with it

  • ਖਰਗੋਸ਼ rabbit
  • ਖੰਡ sugar
  • ਖੇਤ field
  • ਖਿਡੌਣਾ toy

Trace ਖ

ਗ ਗੱਗਾ (gagga)

The "g" of "go".

Hindi letter with the same job: ग.

Words that begin with it

  • ਗਾਂ cow
  • ਗਾਜਰ carrot
  • ਗੁਲਾਬ rose
  • ਗੇਂਦ ball

Trace ਗ

ਘ ਘੱਗਾ (ghagga)

At the start of a word most Punjabi speakers say it like ਕ with a low, dipping tone, not like Hindi घ. So ਘਰ sounds close to "kar" said low. Inside a word it sounds like ਗ and raises the tone before it.

Hindi letter with the same job: घ.

Words that begin with it

  • ਘਰ house
  • ਘੋੜਾ horse
  • ਘੜੀ clock
  • ਘੰਟੀ bell

Trace ਘ

ਙ ਙੰਙਾ (nganga)

The "ng" of "sing". No everyday word begins with it, and it is rare in modern writing. Children learn its name and shape and move on.

Hindi letter with the same job: ङ.

Words

There are no everyday words to list for this letter.

Trace ਙ

Writing this row

In Gurmukhi the line on top is usually written first, from left to right, and the body of the letter is then hung from it. This is the opposite of the habit taught for Hindi, where the top line comes last. A few letters have an open top where the line is broken or missing; copy that gap exactly, because it is part of the letter. More on how the letters are written.
