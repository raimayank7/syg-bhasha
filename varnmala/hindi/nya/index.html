ञ अक्षर: the Hindi letter ञ (nya)

The letter ञ: its sound, the words it appears in, what the shape looks like and how to write it.

ञ
ञ अक्षरno picture word
Trace it

About this letter

ञ is a consonant (व्यंजन) of the Hindi alphabet. It is consonant number 10 of the 33 consonants. Its sound is the "ny" in the middle of "canyon", made through the nose.

A rhyme to remember it

ञ भी अकेला, शब्दों के बीच।Nya too stands alone, in the middle of words.

Say the line together, slowly, and let the child point to the letter each time its sound comes.

Words that have ञ inside them

No everyday Hindi word begins with this letter, so there is no picture word for it. It is met in the middle or at the end of words.

  • चंचल playful (the ञ sound is written with a dot)
  • पंजा paw (written with a dot)
  • ज्ञान knowledge (ञ is hidden inside ज्ञ)

What the shape looks like

A shape a little like ज with an extra curl at the top left, joined to a stick.

How to write it

This letter belongs to the large family that ends in a tall stick on the right. Write it in three moves. First draw the body on the left, starting at the top. Next draw the tall stick from top to bottom so that it closes or meets the body. Last, draw the top line from left to right, like a roof over the whole letter.

Schools differ a little in the handwriting model they teach. If your child's book shows another order of strokes, follow the book. More on how Hindi letters are written.

The same sound in Punjabi

In the Gurmukhi script of Punjabi the matching letter is ਞ, called ਞੰਞਾ (nyanya). See it on the page for the Punjabi row ਚ ਛ ਜ ਝ ਞ.
