ढ़ अक्षर: the Hindi letter ढ़ (rhha)

The letter ढ़: its sound, the words it appears in, what the shape looks like and how to write it.

ढ़
ढ़ अक्षरno picture word
Trace it

About this letter

ढ़ is a dotted letter of the Hindi alphabet. It is taught after the consonants. The dot below the letter is called a nukta. Its sound is the flap of ड़ with breath in it. It is the sound in the middle of पढ़ना.

A rhyme to remember it

ढ़ से पढ़ना, चढ़ना और बूढ़ा।Rhha as in padhna (to read), chadhna (to climb) and boodha (old).

Say the line together, slowly, and let the child point to the letter each time its sound comes.

Words that have ढ़ inside them

No everyday Hindi word begins with this letter, so there is no picture word for it. It is met in the middle or at the end of words.

  • पढ़ना to read
  • चढ़ना to climb
  • बूढ़ा old man
  • सीढ़ी ladder
  • गढ़ fort

What the shape looks like

It is ढ with a dot below. It never begins a word.

How to write it

This letter has no tall stick. It hangs from the top line by a short neck. Start with the neck, a small stroke straight down, and let it flow into the round body without lifting the pencil if you can. Draw the top line last, from left to right, and keep it short: only as wide as the letter.

Schools differ a little in the handwriting model they teach. If your child's book shows another order of strokes, follow the book. More on how Hindi letters are written.

Is there a matching Punjabi letter?

No. The Gurmukhi script has no single letter for ढ़. Punjabi writes such sounds with other letters or does not use them. The comparison of Hindi and Punjabi letters lists all such gaps.
