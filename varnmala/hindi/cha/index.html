च से चम्मच: the Hindi letter च (cha)

The letter च with its picture word चम्मच (spoon), more words, a rhyme, what the shape looks like and how to write it.

च
च से चम्मचचम्मच means spoon
Trace it

About this letter

च is a consonant (व्यंजन) of the Hindi alphabet. It is consonant number 6 of the 33 consonants. Its sound is a light "ch", as in "church" but without a puff of air.

A rhyme to remember it

च से चरखा, सूत काते।Cha for charkha, the wheel that spins thread.

Say the line together, slowly, and let the child point to the letter each time its sound comes.

Words that begin with च

Tap a word to hear it. The first one is the picture word that goes with the letter on this site; your child's school chart may use a different one, and that is fine.

  • चम्मच spoon
  • चरखा spinning wheel
  • चाबी key
  • चींटी ant
  • चश्मा spectacles

What the shape looks like

A flat line goes left from the stick and curves down and back, like an open mouth facing right.

How to write it

This letter belongs to the large family that ends in a tall stick on the right. Write it in three moves. First draw the body on the left, starting at the top. Next draw the tall stick from top to bottom so that it closes or meets the body. Last, draw the top line from left to right, like a roof over the whole letter.

Schools differ a little in the handwriting model they teach. If your child's book shows another order of strokes, follow the book. More on how Hindi letters are written.

The same sound in Punjabi

In the Gurmukhi script of Punjabi the matching letter is ਚ, called ਚੱਚਾ (chacha). See it on the page for the Punjabi row ਚ ਛ ਜ ਝ ਞ.
