आ से आम: the Hindi letter आ (aa)

The letter आ with its picture word आम (mango), more words, a rhyme, what the shape looks like and how to write it.

आ
आ से आमआम means mango
Trace it

About this letter

आ is a vowel (स्वर) of the Hindi alphabet. It is vowel number 2 of the 13 vowels in the school chart. Its sound is the long, open "aa" of "father". Open the mouth wide, as you do for a doctor, and hold the sound for two beats. It is the long partner of अ.

When आ follows a consonant it is not written as a full letter. It shrinks to the sign ◌ा, so क + आ is written का. The full letter is used only at the start of a word or after another vowel.

A rhyme to remember it

आ से आम, गर्मी का इनाम।Aa for aam, the prize of summer.

Say the line together, slowly, and let the child point to the letter each time its sound comes.

Words that begin with आ

Tap a word to hear it. The first one is the picture word that goes with the letter on this site; your child's school chart may use a different one, and that is fine.

  • आम mango
  • आलू potato
  • आग fire
  • आँख eye
  • आकाश sky

What the shape looks like

It is अ with one more stick added on the right. Two sticks side by side mean the long sound.

How to write it

Vowel letters are written in the same order as consonants: the body first, then any stick, then anything that sits above the line, and the top line last. If the letter is built from a simpler one, as आ is built from अ, write the simpler letter fully and then add the extra part.

Schools differ a little in the handwriting model they teach. If your child's book shows another order of strokes, follow the book. More on how Hindi letters are written.

The same sound in Punjabi

In the Gurmukhi script of Punjabi the matching letter is ਆ, written with the vowel carrier . See it on the page for the Punjabi row ੳ ਅ ੲ ਸ ਹ.
