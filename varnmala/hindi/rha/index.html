ड़ अक्षर: the Hindi letter ड़ (rha)

The letter ड़: its sound, the words it appears in, what the shape looks like and how to write it.

ड़
ड़ अक्षरno picture word
Trace it

About this letter

ड़ is a dotted letter of the Hindi alphabet. It is taught after the consonants. The dot below the letter is called a nukta. Its sound is a flap: the curled tongue flicks forward once and strikes the roof of the mouth on the way. It is the sound in the middle of लड़का.

A rhyme to remember it

ड़ से पेड़, लड़का और सड़क।Rha as in ped (tree), ladka (boy) and sadak (road).

Say the line together, slowly, and let the child point to the letter each time its sound comes.

Words that have ड़ inside them

No everyday Hindi word begins with this letter, so there is no picture word for it. It is met in the middle or at the end of words.

  • पेड़ tree
  • लड़का boy
  • सड़क road
  • घोड़ा horse
  • पहाड़ mountain

What the shape looks like

It is ड with a dot below. It never begins a word.

How to write it

This letter has no tall stick. It hangs from the top line by a short neck. Start with the neck, a small stroke straight down, and let it flow into the round body without lifting the pencil if you can. Draw the top line last, from left to right, and keep it short: only as wide as the letter.

Schools differ a little in the handwriting model they teach. If your child's book shows another order of strokes, follow the book. More on how Hindi letters are written.

The same sound in Punjabi

In the Gurmukhi script of Punjabi the matching letter is ੜ, called ੜਾੜਾ (rara (hard)). See it on the page for the Punjabi row ਯ ਰ ਲ ਵ ੜ.
