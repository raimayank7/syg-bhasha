Letter tracing sheets

Choose a script and where to start. Each row has the letter in black, three grey copies to trace and room to write two more.

What is on a sheet

Each letter gets one row. On the left is the letter in solid black, the model. Next come three copies in grey outline to go over with a pencil. Then two empty spaces follow, where the child writes the letter without help. At the end of the row a small label gives the picture word for Hindi letters, or the name of the letter for Punjabi, so that an adult who does not read the script can still help.

How many letters at a time?

Six letters to a page gives the largest writing and suits children of four and five. Eight or ten letters fit children who already hold a pencil well. A whole alphabet is nine pages of six in Hindi and seven in Punjabi. Do not print them all at once: one page finished with care teaches more than five pages rushed.

Printing tips

  • Print on A4 paper in portrait. Only the white sheet is printed; the menus are left out.
  • Turn off "Headers and footers" in the print window for a clean page.
  • For daily practice, slide one printed sheet into a clear plastic folder and write on the folder with a whiteboard marker. It wipes clean and saves paper.
  • The shape of the letters comes from the Hindi or Punjabi font on your device. A phone and a computer may draw a letter slightly differently; both are correct forms.

Before the pencil

A child who cannot yet draw a straight line or a circle is not ready for letter sheets. Begin with standing lines, sleeping lines, slanting lines and circles on plain paper. Then use the on-screen tracing pad with a finger, which needs no pencil grip, and come to these sheets after that. The guide teaching the alphabet at home sets out the whole path.
