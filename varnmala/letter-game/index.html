Letter game

Four choices, one right answer, no timer.

0 right out of 0

How to play

Hindi game. The box shows a line with a gap, such as __ से अनार. The child says the word aloud, listens for its first sound, and taps the letter that makes it. The right letter turns green; a wrong tap shows which one was right.

Punjabi game. The box shows the name of a letter, such as ਕੱਕਾ (kakka), and the child taps the letter that carries that name. This is how Punjabi children are tested in the first months of school, because every Gurmukhi letter has its own name.

The next question comes by itself after a moment. The count at the bottom shows right answers out of all answers so far. It is not saved, and there is nothing to win or lose.

Why a game helps

Reciting the alphabet in order proves memory of a song, not knowledge of letters. A child who can pick भ out of four shapes, in no fixed order, truly knows it. The wrong choices in this game are other letters of the same script, so mistakes show which shapes are being mixed. If भ and म keep getting swapped, open their letter pages and compare the shapes side by side.

Playing without the screen

  • Write six letters on paper slips, spread them on the floor and call out a picture word. The child jumps to the right letter.
  • On a walk, hunt for one chosen letter on shop boards and number plates.
  • Trace a letter on the child's back with a finger and let them guess it.

For writing practice, go to the tracing pad or print tracing sheets.
