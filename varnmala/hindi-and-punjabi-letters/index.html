Hindi and Punjabi letters side by side

Hindi and Punjabi are sister languages, and their scripts are cousins: both descend from the old Brahmi script and both arrange their letters by the place of the sound in the mouth. A reader of one can learn the other in a few weeks. This page puts the letters side by side and points out the traps.

Shapes that are close cousins

Some pairs look so alike that a Hindi reader can guess them at once: ग and ਗ, ट and ਟ, ठ and ਠ, अ and ਅ, ढ and ਢ. These are a good place to start, because they give quick wins.

False friends: same look, different sound

A few Punjabi letters look like a Hindi letter but stand for another sound. These cause most mistakes for people who read Hindi first:

  • ਸ is "s", but its shape is very close to Hindi म (m).
  • ਧ is "dh", but its shape is very close to Hindi प (p).
  • ਬ is "b", but its shape is very close to Hindi घ (gh).

Inside Gurmukhi itself some letters differ only by a small stroke, and a newcomer mixes them: ਸ and ਮ, ਪ and ਧ, ਬ and ਥ, ਹ and ਰ. Learn each pair with a word: ਸੇਬ (apple) and ਮੋਰ (peacock), ਪਾਣੀ (water) and ਧੁੱਪ (sunshine), ਬੱਕਰੀ (goat) and ਥਾਲੀ (plate).

What Hindi has and Punjabi does not

  • A separate letter for every vowel. Punjabi builds its vowels on three carriers, ੳ ਅ ੲ.
  • The vowel ऋ and the letter ष. Punjabi writes such words with ਰਿ and ਸ਼.
  • The joined letters क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र, and half letters in general. Punjabi writes most consonants in full and joins only three letters, ਹ ਰ ਵ, in a small form at the foot of another letter.

What Punjabi has and Hindi does not

  • Tones. ਘ ਝ ਢ ਧ ਭ are not said as breathy sounds. At the start of a word they sound like ਕ ਚ ਟ ਤ ਪ with a low tone. Hindi has no tones.
  • A doubling sign. The small mark ੱ (addhak) above a letter doubles the consonant that follows: ਪੱਕਾ. Hindi writes the letter twice, with a half form: पक्का.
  • Two nasal signs with a clear rule. ੰ (tippi) and ਂ (bindi) are used with different vowels.
  • The letter ੜ as a full member of the alphabet. Hindi makes the same sound with a dotted letter, ड़.
  • A name for every letter, such as ਕੱਕਾ and ਮੰਮਾ.

The same word in both scripts

  • water: पानी, ਪਾਣੀ
  • house: घर, ਘਰ
  • milk: दूध, ਦੁੱਧ
  • hand: हाथ, ਹੱਥ
  • parrot: तोता, ਤੋਤਾ
  • king: राजा, ਰਾਜਾ

Notice how often Punjabi shortens a long Hindi vowel and doubles the next consonant instead. To go further, open the Punjabi alphabet row by row, or the Hindi varnmala letter by letter.
