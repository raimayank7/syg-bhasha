How many letters are there in Hindi and Punjabi?

Ask three Hindi books how many letters the alphabet has and you may get three answers. None of them is wrong. They count the same letters and differ only in which extra ones they include. This page shows exactly what is inside each total.

The parts everyone agrees on

  • 11 vowels: अ आ इ ई उ ऊ ऋ ए ऐ ओ औ
  • 33 consonants: the twenty-five from क to म, then य र ल व and श ष स ह

That gives 44, and a book that says "44 letters" has stopped here.

The parts that are added

  • 2 extra signs: अं (anusvara) and अः (visarga). They are not true vowels, but charts print them after the vowels. With them the total is 46.
  • 2 dotted letters: ड़ and ढ़, which are needed for common words like पेड़ and पढ़ना. With them the total is 48.
  • 4 joined letters: क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र. Each is two consonants written as one shape. With them the total is 52.

So the common counts line up like this: 44 (vowels and consonants only), 46 (with अं and अः), 48 (with ड़ and ढ़) and 52 (with the four joined letters). This site shows all 52, because that is what a child will meet on most school charts today.

Why the joined letters are counted at all

Strictly, क्ष is just क् and ष joined, and Hindi has many other joins that nobody counts. These four earned a place because their shapes do not look like their parts. A child cannot work out ज्ञ from ज and ञ; it has to be learnt as a picture.

Letters that are not on the chart

You will also see क़ ख़ ग़ ज़ फ़, with a dot below, in words from Urdu, Persian and English, and the sign ऑ in English words like डॉक्टर. They are used in print every day but are not usually counted in the varnmala. The half-moon ँ (chandrabindu) is a sign, not a letter.

What should my child learn?

Follow the school. If the class chart has 52 boxes, teach 52. If it has 48, do not worry about the four joined letters yet. In an exam, the answer wanted is the one in the child's own textbook; the lists above let you check which letters that book has left out.

And Punjabi?

The Punjabi alphabet in the Gurmukhi script has a much firmer count. Its traditional name, ਪੈਂਤੀ (painti), means thirty-five: seven rows of five letters, from ੳ to ੜ. Six more letters were added later by putting a dot at the foot of an existing letter: ਸ਼ ਖ਼ ਗ਼ ਜ਼ ਫ਼ ਲ਼. That makes 41. The vowel signs, the nasal signs and the doubling sign are counted separately and are on the page about Punjabi vowel signs.
