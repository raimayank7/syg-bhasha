Open the Punjabi alphabet

What is a varnmala?

वर्णमाला means "garland of letters": वर्ण is a letter or sound and माला is a garland. It is the Hindi word for the alphabet. The letters are strung in a fixed order, vowels first and consonants after, and that order follows the way sounds are made in the mouth. Children first learn to recite it, then to recognise each letter out of order, then to write it. Joining letters with vowel signs to read words comes after that.

How to use this site

  1. Meet the letter. Open a letter page, hear the sound and say the picture word: अ से अनार.
  2. Say the rhyme. Each page has a short line to chant together.
  3. Look at the shape. Read what the letter looks like and which letters it resembles.
  4. Trace it. Use a finger on the tracing pad, then a pencil on a printed sheet.
  5. Play. Check what has stuck with the letter game.

For parents and teachers

Teaching the alphabet at home gives a gentle routine for children of three to six. How to write the letters explains the order of strokes in both scripts. How many letters are there? answers the question every parent asks when two books disagree, and Hindi and Punjabi letters side by side helps families who use both languages.

Questions

My child's chart has a different picture word. Which is right?

Both. There is no official list of picture words. Any familiar word that begins with the letter does the job. Use the one from the school book so that home and school match.

Why do some letters have no picture word?

No Hindi word begins with ङ, ञ, ण, ड़ or ढ़, and none begins with the sound अः. Their pages show words that carry them inside instead.

Does the site read the letters aloud?

It uses the voice of your own device. Most phones have a Hindi voice. A Punjabi voice is rare, so the Punjabi buttons may stay silent or sound wrong; the names are also written in English letters for that reason.
