Dhatu rupa: verb tables

A Sanskrit verb grows from a root, called a धातु (dhatu). From the root पठ्, "to read", come पठति (he reads), पठामि (I read), पठिष्यति (he will read) and hundreds of other forms. The full table of a root in one tense is its धातुरूप (dhatu-rupa).

The verb ending tells you the person and the number, so the pronoun is often left out: पठामि alone means "I read". There are three persons and three numbers, giving nine forms in each tense. Note that Sanskrit lists the persons in the opposite order to English: "he" first, "you" second, "I" last. Unlike Hindi, the verb does not change with gender: बालकः पठति, बालिका पठति.

The tenses and moods are called लकार (lakara), because their technical names all begin with "l". Five are taught first.

How the forms are built

  • Present: root + अ + ending: पठ् + अ + ति = पठति.
  • Future: the same endings as the present, with इष्य (or स्य) put in: पठ् + इष्य + ति = पठिष्यति. So if you know the present, you know the future.
  • Past: अ is added in front and the endings are shortened: अ + पठ् + अ + त् = अपठत्. That front अ is the mark of the past.
  • Command: the plain पठ is "read!" to one person; पठतु is the polite "please read" used with भवान्.

Roots such as गम्, पा, दृश्, स्था change their look in the present (गच्छति, पिबति, पश्यति, तिष्ठति) but then take the ordinary endings: गच्छामि (I go), पिबसि (you drink), पश्यन्ति (they see). A few, such as अस्, कृ, दा, श्रु, ज्ञा, belong to other classes and have their own tables; the commonest forms of अस् are अस्ति (he is), असि (you are), अस्मि (I am), सन्ति (they are).

Parasmaipada and atmanepada

The endings above (-ति, -तः, -न्ति) are called परस्मैपद. Some roots take a second set, आत्मनेपद (-ते, -एते, -अन्ते): लभते (he obtains), वर्तते (he is), मन्ये (I think). You will meet both in the shlokas: श्रद्धावाँल्लभते ज्ञानम्, "the one with faith obtains knowledge".

How to learn

Chant the present table until it is automatic: पठति पठतः पठन्ति, पठसि पठथः पठथ, पठामि पठावः पठामः. Then put another root through the same endings: लिखति लिखतः लिखन्ति... Finally, join subject and verb correctly: सः पठति, तौ पठतः, ते पठन्ति, त्वं पठसि, अहं पठामि, वयं पठामः.
