Classroom and polite sentences

20 sentences in Sanskrit, each with a roman reading and the meaning in Hindi and English.

A Sanskrit class conducted in Sanskrit needs only a few dozen sentences, and they are a pleasure to use because they are so compact. A single verb form is a whole polite request: आगच्छतु, "please come"; उपविशतु, "please sit".

This ending, -अतु, is the request form that goes with the respectful भवान् or भवती. It is the right form for a teacher, an elder or a stranger, and teachers use it to students too. To several people it becomes -अन्तु: सर्वे उपविशन्तु, "everybody please sit". The bare command to one friend drops the ending: आगच्छ, उपविश, पठ.

The other key word is कृपया, "kindly", the same word that Hindi uses. And for "sorry", Sanskrit says क्षम्यताम्, "may it be forgiven".

  • आगच्छतु।

    āgachchhatu.

    आइए।

    Please come.

  • उपविशतु।

    upavishatu.

    बैठिए।

    Please sit down.

  • उत्तिष्ठतु।

    uttishthatu.

    खड़े होइए।

    Please stand up.

  • कृपया पुनः वदतु।

    kripayā punah vadatu.

    कृपया फिर से बोलिए।

    Please say it again.

  • शनैः वदतु।

    shanaih vadatu.

    धीरे बोलिए।

    Please speak slowly.

  • पुस्तकम् उद्घाटयतु।

    pustakam udghātayatu.

    पुस्तक खोलिए।

    Please open the book.

  • पठतु।

    pathatu.

    पढ़िए।

    Please read.

  • लिखतु।

    likhatu.

    लिखिए।

    Please write.

  • अहं न जानामि।

    aham na jānāmi.

    मुझे नहीं पता।

    I do not know.

  • अहं न अवगच्छामि।

    aham na avagachchhāmi.

    मुझे समझ नहीं आया।

    I do not understand.

  • एकः प्रश्नः अस्ति।

    ekah prashnah asti.

    एक प्रश्न है।

    I have a question.

  • अस्य अर्थः कः?

    asya arthah kah?

    इसका अर्थ क्या है?

    What is the meaning of this?

  • क्षम्यताम्।

    kshamyatām.

    क्षमा कीजिए।

    Excuse me; I am sorry.

  • चिन्ता मास्तु।

    chintā māstu.

    चिंता मत कीजिए।

    Do not worry.

  • अस्तु।

    astu.

    ठीक है।

    All right; so be it.

  • आम्। / न।

    ām, / na.

    हाँ। / नहीं।

    Yes. / No.

  • बहु शोभनम्।

    bahu shobhanam.

    बहुत अच्छा।

    Very good.

  • सम्यक् उक्तम्।

    samyak uktam.

    ठीक कहा।

    Well said.

  • किम् अहम् अन्तः आगच्छानि?

    kim aham antah āgachchhāni?

    क्या मैं अंदर आ सकता हूँ?

    May I come in?

  • श्वः मिलामः।

    shvah milāmah.

    कल मिलते हैं।

    See you tomorrow.

Asking questions

Sanskrit question words begin with क, as Hindi ones do: किम् (what), कः / का (who, masculine and feminine), कुत्र (where), कदा (when), कथम् (how), किमर्थम् (why), कति (how many). A yes-or-no question puts किम् first, like the Hindi क्या: किं भवान् संस्कृतं जानाति?, "Do you know Sanskrit?" The answer is आम्, जानामि or न जानामि.

More of these small unchanging words are on the avyaya page.
