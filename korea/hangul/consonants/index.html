How to read the table

The syllable column shows each consonant followed by the vowel ㅏ ("a"), so you can see the letter in a real block. The romanization is the Revised Romanization of Korea, made by a computer package and cross-checked with a second package. Where two letters are shown (g / k), the sound changes with the position: the first at the start of a word, the second at the end of a syllable.

Three groups

Many consonants come in a plain, an aspirated (with a puff of air) and a tense (with no puff) form: ㄱ, ㅋ and ㄲ; ㄷ, ㅌ and ㄸ; ㅂ, ㅍ and ㅃ; ㅈ, ㅊ and ㅉ. English does not distinguish these, which is why they are the hardest sounds for many learners. Listen to a native speaker to learn them.
