About Chuseok

Chuseok (추석) is the harvest festival, held on the fifteenth day of the eighth lunar month and often called the Korean Thanksgiving. Families gather to give thanks for the harvest and to remember their ancestors with a memorial ceremony (charye) and a visit to the graves. The traditional food is songpyeon, half-moon rice cakes steamed over pine needles, along with jeon (fried pancakes) and fruit. Many Koreans travel to their hometowns, so roads and trains are very crowded and tickets sell out early.

Planning a visit

Shops, banks and many restaurants close for the main days, while tourist places and convenience stores are often open. The holiday is three days long and the days around it are busy. See all Korean holidays and the etiquette guide.
