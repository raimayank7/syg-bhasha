How the dates are found

Seollal is the first day of the first lunar month, Buddha's Birthday is the eighth day of the fourth lunar month, and Chuseok is the fifteenth day of the eighth lunar month. We compute them with a Korean lunar calendar package and compare them with the Chinese lunar calendar. Seollal and Chuseok are each a three-day holiday (the day before, the day and the day after); if one of these days falls on a Sunday or another holiday, a substitute holiday may follow. We do not compute the substitute days, and the law can change.

The Korean date of Seollal is one day later than the Chinese New Year date in 2027 (2027-02-07, not 2027-02-06) and 2028 (2028-01-27, not 2028-01-26) (the two calendars are calculated for different places, so a new moon near midnight can fall on different days). Check the official Korean calendar before you plan travel.

Official source

The official calendar of lunar dates in Korea is published by the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute, and public holidays are set by Korean law. Check them before you book.
