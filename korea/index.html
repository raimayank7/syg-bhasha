How Hangul works

Each Korean syllable is a block made of two or three letters: a first consonant, a vowel, and sometimes a final consonant. The builder shows the block, its Revised Romanization and its Unicode value, and the rule that joins the letters. Read the story of Hangul to see why the alphabet is so regular.

Culture

The holiday dates for Seollal and Chuseok are computed for ten years. The guides cover speech levels, names and titles and etiquette.

Questions

How do I type Hangul?

Add a Korean keyboard to your phone or computer. The builder here is for learning how syllables are put together.

Is the romanization official?

It follows the Revised Romanization of Korea, made by a package. Real names and signs may use older spellings.
